Recognize and write equations of parallel lines.

02 · WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

Distinct parallel lines have equal slopes.

Parallel nonvertical lines rise and run at the same rate, so their slopes are equal. Their y-intercepts must be different if the lines are distinct.

VISUAL GRAPH

Equal slopes create parallel lines.

Compare y = 2x + 1 and y = 2x − 3. Both have slope 2, but different y-intercepts, so the lines keep the same direction and never meet.

Math cross-checkFor y = 2x + 1, x = −1, 0, 1 gives y = −1, 1, 3. For y = 2x − 3, the same x-values give y = −5, −3, −1. Each line has slope 2, while their y-intercepts are 1 and −3, so they are distinct parallel lines.
DESMOS STRATEGY

Use a graphing tool to verify a parallel relationship.

Enter

y = 2x + 1
y = 2x - 3

Look for

Two lines with the same steepness and direction, separated vertically, with no intersection.

Use it to answer

Confirm that two equations represent distinct parallel lines when their graphs have equal slope but different intercepts.

Why it works

For nonvertical lines, equal slopes mean equal rates of change. Different y-intercepts place those equal-slope lines at different vertical positions, so they never intersect.

Faster or not?

Comparing slopes algebraically is usually faster. Desmos is useful as a visual check, especially when equations first need rearranging.
