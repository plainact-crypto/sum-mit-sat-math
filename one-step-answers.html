01 · SKILL CHECK

Warm-up solutions

These match questions 1–3 on the Practice Problems page.

1

Solve: x + 6 = 14

Answer: x = 8
x + 6 − 6 = 14 − 6x = 8

Subtract 6 from both sides to undo the +6.

2

Solve: x − 5 = 7

Answer: x = 12
x − 5 + 5 = 7 + 5x = 12

Add 5 to both sides to undo the −5.

3

Solve: 4x = 36

Answer: x = 9
4x / 4 = 36 / 4x = 9

Divide both sides by 4.

10

Solve: (1/2)x = −3

Answer: x = −6
2 · (1/2)x = 2(−3)x = −6

Multiplying by 2 undoes multiplication by 1/2.

11

Answer: x = 18
3(x / 3) = 6(3)x = 18
03 · SAT-STYLE PRACTICE

Test-style solutions

These match questions 12–16.

12

If 7x = 56, what is the value of x?

Answer: B) 8
x = 56 / 7x = 8
SAT tip: When the variable is multiplied by a number, divide by that number.
13

A number decreased by 12 is 19. What is the number?

Answer: 31
x − 12 = 19x = 19 + 12x = 31

“Decreased by 12” translates to subtract 12.

14

A club charges a $9 discount from its regular monthly price p. The discounted price is $15. What is p?

Answer: p = 24
p − 9 = 15p = 24

Regular price − discount = discounted price.

15

Which value of x satisfies −5x = 45?

Answer: D) −9
x = 45 / (−5)x = −9
16

If x/6 = 7, what is x?

Answer: 42
x = 7 · 6x = 42

Challenge solutions

17

Solve: −2.5x = 14

Answer: x = −5.6
x = 14 / (−2.5)x = −5.6

The process is unchanged even when the coefficient is a decimal.

18

Solve: x / (−3/2) = 6

Answer: x = −9
x = 6(−3/2)x = −18/2x = −9

Multiply both sides by −3/2 because x is being divided by −3/2.
