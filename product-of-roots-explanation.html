Product of Roots
Advanced Math
01 · LESSON OBJECTIVE

Find the product of a quadratic’s roots without solving for each root.

For a quadratic in standard form, the coefficients tell you the product of the roots directly.

02 · WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

Write the quadratic as ax²+bx+c=0 first.

The coefficient rule only works when you correctly identify a, b, and c from standard form.

03 · KEY DEFINITIONS

Root

An x-value that makes the quadratic equal zero.

Product

The result of multiplying the two roots.

Standard Form

ax²+bx+c=0, with a≠0.

04 · CORE RULE

For ax²+bx+c=0, the product of the roots is c/a.

r₁r₂=c/aa≠0

This follows from a(x−r₁)(x−r₂): the constant term is ar₁r₂, so c=ar₁r₂.

05 · HOW IT WORKS

06 · WORKED EXAMPLE 1

Find the product of the roots of x²−7x+10=0.

a=1, c=10r₁r₂=10/1=10
Check(x−2)(x−5)=0, so the roots are 2 and 5 and 2·5=10.
07 · WORKED EXAMPLE 2

Find the product of the roots of 3x²+12x−8=0.

a=3, c=−8r₁r₂=−8/3

No root solving is needed.

The roots of 2x²+kx+m=0 have product 6. Find m.

m/2=6m=12

10 · COMMON MISTAKES
Using −c/a

The product is c/a, with no extra negative sign.

Using b

The x-coefficient b is used in the sum-of-roots rule, not the product rule.

Skipping standard form

Move every term to one side before reading a and c.

Forgetting signs

A negative constant gives a negative product when a is positive.

11 · EXAM STRATEGY

If the question asks only for the product, use c/a immediately.

Do not factor or use the quadratic formula unless another part of the problem requires the individual roots.

13 · LESSON RECAP
  1. Write the equation as ax²+bx+c=0.
  2. The product of the roots is c/a.
  3. Keep the sign of c.
  4. Use the rule directly when individual roots are not needed.
  5. Use factoring or graph zeros only as a check when useful.
