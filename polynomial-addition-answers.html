Polynomial Addition

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. Add: (3x + 4) + (2x - 7)

Answer: A) 5x - 3
Combine like terms: 3x + 2x = 5x and 4 - 7 = -3.

2. Add: (5y² - 2y) + (3y² + 6y)

Answer: A) 8y² + 4y
Add matching powers: 5y² + 3y² = 8y² and -2y + 6y = 4y.

3. Add: (4a² + 3a - 5) + (a² - 7a + 2)

Answer: A) 5a² - 4a - 3
Combine coefficients by degree: 4+1=5, 3-7=-4, and -5+2=-3.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. Add: (2x³ - 5x + 1) + (7x³ + 3x - 4)

Answer: A) 9x³ - 2x - 3
The cubic terms give 9x³, the x-terms give -2x, and the constants give -3.

5. Add: (6m² + 4m - 9) + (-2m² + 5m + 3)

Answer: A) 4m² + 9m - 6
6m²-2m²=4m², 4m+5m=9m, and -9+3=-6.

6. Add: (-3p³ + 2p² - p) + (5p³ - 7p² + 4p)

Answer: A) 2p³ - 5p² + 3p
Add each degree separately: -3+5=2, 2-7=-5, and -1+4=3.

7. Add: (x⁴ + 2x² - 8) + (3x³ - 5x² + 6)

Answer: A) x⁴ + 3x³ - 3x² - 2
Keep unmatched x⁴ and x³ terms, then 2x²-5x²=-3x² and -8+6=-2.

8. Add: (0.5t² + 1.2t - 3) + (1.5t² - 0.7t + 4)

Answer: A) 2t² + 0.5t + 1
0.5+1.5=2, 1.2-0.7=0.5, and -3+4=1.

9. Add: (1/2)x² + (3/4)x + 2 and (1/3)x² - (1/4)x - 5

Answer: A) (5/6)x² + (1/2)x - 3
1/2+1/3=5/6, 3/4-1/4=1/2, and 2-5=-3.

10. If P(x)=4x²-3x+7 and Q(x)=-x²+5x-2, find P(x)+Q(x).

Answer: A) 3x² + 2x + 5
Add corresponding coefficients: 4-1=3, -3+5=2, and 7-2=5.

11. Add: (7r⁵ - 2r³ + r) + (-4r⁵ + 6r² - 3r)

Answer: A) 3r⁵ - 2r³ + 6r² - 2r
7r⁵-4r⁵=3r⁵; the r³ and r² terms have no partners; r-3r=-2r.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. Which expression is equivalent to (3x² - 4x + 9) + (5x² + x - 12)?

Answer: A) 8x² - 3x - 3
3x²+5x²=8x², -4x+x=-3x, and 9-12=-3.

13. The lengths of two trail sections are (2x²+3x+1) km and (x²-5x+4) km. What is their total length?

Answer: A) 3x² - 2x + 5 km
Add the two length expressions: (2+1)x²+(3-5)x+(1+4)=3x²-2x+5 km.

14. For A(x)=2x³-x²+4 and B(x)=-5x³+6x²-7x, what is A(x)+B(x)?

Answer: A) -3x³ + 5x² - 7x + 4
2-5=-3 for x³, -1+6=5 for x², the x-term is -7x, and the constant is 4.

15. A school records (4n²+2n+18) morning visits and (3n²-5n+12) afternoon visits. Which polynomial represents the total?

Answer: A) 7n² - 3n + 30
Add morning and afternoon counts: 4+3=7, 2-5=-3, and 18+12=30.

16. If the sum of two polynomials is 6x³+2x²-5x+9 and one polynomial is 2x³-x²+x+4, which polynomial could be the other?

Answer: A) 4x³ + 3x² - 6x + 5
Subtract the known polynomial from the sum: (6-2)x³ + (2-(-1))x² + (-5-1)x + (9-4) = 4x³+3x²-6x+5. Adding it back reproduces the stated sum.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. Add: (2a⁴b - 3a²b² + 5ab³) + (-7a⁴b + 8a²b² - 2ab³)

Answer: A) -5a⁴b + 5a²b² + 3ab³
Combine identical monomials: 2-7=-5, -3+8=5, and 5-2=3.

18. For P(x)=kx²+3x-4 and Q(x)=(5-k)x²-8x+9, simplify P(x)+Q(x).

Answer: A) 5x² - 5x + 5
The x² coefficient is k+(5-k)=5, the x coefficient is 3-8=-5, and the constant is -4+9=5. The parameter k cancels.
