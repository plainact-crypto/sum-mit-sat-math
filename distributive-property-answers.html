Distributive Property

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. Expand: 3(x + 4)

Answer: A) 3x + 12
Multiply 3 by each term: 3·x + 3·4 = 3x + 12.

2. Expand: 5(2a - 3)

Answer: A) 10a - 15
5·2a = 10a and 5·(-3) = -15.

3. Expand: -2(y + 6)

Answer: A) -2y - 12
The negative factor multiplies both terms: -2·y + (-2)·6 = -2y - 12.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. Expand: 4(3x + 2)

Answer: A) 12x + 8
4·3x + 4·2 = 12x + 8.

5. Expand: -3(2m - 5)

Answer: A) -6m + 15
-3·2m = -6m and -3·(-5) = +15.

6. Expand: x(x + 7)

Answer: A) x² + 7x
x·x = x² and x·7 = 7x.

7. Expand: (1/2)(8p + 6)

Answer: A) 4p + 3
Half of 8p is 4p and half of 6 is 3.

8. Expand: 0.4(5t - 10)

Answer: A) 2t - 4
0.4·5t = 2t and 0.4·(-10) = -4.

9. Simplify: 2(3x + 4) + x

Answer: A) 7x + 8
Distribute first: 6x + 8 + x. Combine like terms to get 7x + 8.

10. Simplify: 5(a - 2) - 2a

Answer: A) 3a - 10
5a - 10 - 2a = 3a - 10.

11. Which factored form equals 12x + 18?

Answer: A) 6(2x + 3)
Factoring out 6 gives 12x ÷ 6 = 2x and 18 ÷ 6 = 3. Expanding 6(2x+3) returns 12x+18.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. Rectangle area with width x + 3 and length 4

Answer: A) 4x + 12
Area = length × width = 4(x+3) = 4x+12 square units.

13. Equivalent to -5(2x - 7)

Answer: A) -10x + 35
-5·2x = -10x and -5·(-7) = +35.

14. If 3(2x + k) = 6x + 15 for every x, find k

Answer: B) 5
Expand the left side: 6x + 3k. Matching constants gives 3k=15, so k=5. Substitution checks it: 3(2x+5)=6x+15.

15. Simplify: 2(4y - 3) - 3(y + 2)

Answer: A) 5y - 12
8y-6-3y-6 = 5y-12.

16. Total cost after reducing each of n $18 items by $3

Answer: A) 15n
Each item costs 18-3=$15 after the discount, so n items cost 15n dollars.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. Simplify: -2[3x - 4(1 - x)]

Answer: A) -14x + 8
Inside the brackets, 4(1-x)=4-4x, so 3x-(4-4x)=7x-4. Multiplying by -2 gives -14x+8.

18. Equivalent to 6x² - 9x

Answer: A) 3x(2x - 3)
The greatest common factor is 3x: 6x² ÷ 3x = 2x and -9x ÷ 3x = -3. Expanding 3x(2x-3) verifies 6x²-9x.
