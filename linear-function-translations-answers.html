Linear Function Translations

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. Shift y = 2x + 1 up 4 units

Answer: A) y = 2x + 5
A vertical shift up 4 adds 4 to every output: y = (2x + 1) + 4 = 2x + 5.

2. Shift y = −3x + 7 down 5 units

Answer: A) y = −3x + 2
A downward shift of 5 subtracts 5 from the output: y = −3x + 7 − 5 = −3x + 2.

3. Shift f(x) = x − 2 right 3 units

Answer: A) y = x − 5
A right shift by 3 replaces x with x − 3: f(x − 3) = (x − 3) − 2 = x − 5.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. Shift f(x) = 4x + 1 left 2 units

Answer: A) y = 4x + 9
A left shift by 2 replaces x with x + 2: f(x + 2) = 4(x + 2) + 1 = 4x + 9.

5. New y-intercept after shifting up 3

Answer: A) 9
The original y-intercept is 6. Shifting the whole line up 3 gives 6 + 3 = 9.

6. Shift y = 0.5x − 4 down 6 units

Answer: A) y = 0.5x − 10
Subtract 6 from the output: y = 0.5x − 4 − 6 = 0.5x − 10.

7. Shift f(x) = 3x − 5 right 4 units

Answer: A) y = 3x − 17
Use f(x − 4): 3(x − 4) − 5 = 3x − 12 − 5 = 3x − 17.

8. Shift f(x) = −x + 2 left 5 units

Answer: A) y = −x − 3
Use f(x + 5): −(x + 5) + 2 = −x − 5 + 2 = −x − 3.

9. Compare y = 2x + 3 and y = 2x − 4

Answer: A) Down 7 units
The slope stays 2 while the intercept changes from 3 to −4, a change of −7. The graph moved down 7 units.

10. Compare f(x) = x + 8 and g(x) = x + 1

Answer: A) Shifted down 7 units
g(x) = f(x) − 7 because x + 1 = (x + 8) − 7. So g is f shifted down 7 units.

11. Move y-intercept 5 to 11 without changing slope

Answer: A) Up 6 units
The intercept increases by 11 − 5 = 6, so the line is shifted vertically upward 6 units.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. Lower the starting fee in C = 2.5m + 6 by $2

Answer: A) C = 2.5m + 4
The per-unit rate 2.5 stays unchanged. Only the initial value changes: 6 − 2 = 4.

13. Shift T = 3t + 10 right 2 units

Answer: A) T = 3(t − 2) + 10
A horizontal shift right 2 replaces the input t with t − 2.

14. Shift point (0, 2) left 3 units

Answer: A) (−3, 2)
A left shift subtracts 3 from the x-coordinate and leaves the y-coordinate unchanged: (0 − 3, 2) = (−3, 2).

15. Evaluate g(4) when g(x) = f(x) + 5 and f(4) = 7

Answer: A) 12
g(4) = f(4) + 5 = 7 + 5 = 12.

16. Point movement for g(x) = f(x − 6)

Answer: A) (8, −1)
Replacing x with x − 6 shifts the graph right 6 units. The point (2, −1) therefore moves to (2 + 6, −1) = (8, −1).
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. Find h in g(x) = f(x + h) − 4

Answer: A) 7
With f(x) = 2x − 3, g(x) = 2(x + h) − 3 − 4 = 2x + 2h − 7. Match this to 2x + 7: 2h − 7 = 7, so 2h = 14 and h = 7.

18. Right a units, then up 5 units

Answer: A) 4
Shift y = −3x + 2 right a units: y = −3(x − a) + 2 = −3x + 3a + 2. Then move up 5: y = −3x + 3a + 7. Match the final intercept 19: 3a + 7 = 19, so 3a = 12 and a = 4.
