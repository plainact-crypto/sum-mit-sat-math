Reversing the Inequality Sign

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. Solve −3x > 12

Answer: B) x < −4
Divide both sides by −3. Because the divisor is negative, reverse the inequality: x < −4. Check with x=−5: 15>12.

2. Solve x/(−5) ≤ 2

Answer: B) x ≥ −10
Multiply both sides by −5 and reverse the inequality: x ≥ −10. At x=−10, the left side equals 2.

3. Solve −2(x − 1) ≥ 8

Answer: B) x ≤ −3
Divide by −2 and reverse: x−1 ≤ −4. Add 1: x ≤ −3. At x=−3, both sides equal 8.

Answer: B) x > −3
Subtract 7: −4x<12. Divide by −4 and reverse: x>−3.

5. Solve 5 − 3x ≥ 20

Answer: B) x ≤ −5

7. Solve 9 − 2x ≤ 1

Answer: B) x ≥ 4

8. Solve −0.5x > 3

9. Solve (−x)/4 ≥ −2

Answer: B) x ≤ 8

10. Solve 2x − 5 < −x + 7

Answer: A) x < 4
Add x and 5: 3x<12. Divide by positive 3: x<4.

11. Solve −7x + 4 ≤ 18

Subtract 4: −7x≤14. Divide by −7 and reverse: x≥−2.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. Practice-book budget

Answer: C) 10
Keeping at least $40 gives 120−8x≥40. Then −8x≥−80. Divide by −8 and reverse: x≤10. The greatest whole-number value is 10 books.

13. Decreasing temperature

Answer: B) h > 6
Model the temperature by 18−2.5h<3. Then −2.5h<−15. Divide by −2.5 and reverse: h>6 hours.

14. Freight platform

Answer: C) 8
At least 300 kg must remain: 900−75p≥300. Then −75p≥−600. Divide by −75 and reverse: p≤8. The greatest possible value is 8.

15. Draining tank

Answer: B) m < 10
More than 15 liters means 50−3.5m>15. Then −3.5m>−35. Divide by −3.5 and reverse: m<10 minutes.

16. Game score

Answer: B) w ≥ 7
At most 72 points means 100−4w≤72. Then −4w≤−28. Divide by −4 and reverse: w≥7.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. Solve −3(2x − 5) > 4x + 25

Answer: B) x < −1
Expand: −6x+15>4x+25. Subtract 4x and 15: −10x>10. Divide by −10 and reverse: x<−1.

18. Solve (−5/2)(x − 4) < 15

Answer: B) x > −2
Multiply both sides by −2/5 and reverse: x−4>−6. Add 4: x>−2.
