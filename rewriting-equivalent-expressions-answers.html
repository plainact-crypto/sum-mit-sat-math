Rewriting Equivalent Expressions

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. Simplify: 3(x + 4)

Answer: B) 3x + 12
Distribute 3 to both terms: 3·x + 3·4 = 3x + 12.

2. Simplify: 7x + 2x - 5

Answer: A) 9x - 5
Combine the like x-terms: 7x + 2x = 9x. The constant remains -5.

3. Factor completely: 10y + 15

Answer: C) 5(2y + 3)
The greatest common factor is 5. Dividing each term by 5 gives 2y + 3; expanding confirms 10y + 15.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. Simplify: 4(a - 3) + 2a

Answer: A) 6a - 12
Distribute first: 4a - 12 + 2a. Combine like terms to get 6a - 12.

5. Simplify: 5m + 8 - 2(m - 1)

Answer: C) 3m + 10
Distribute -2: 5m + 8 - 2m + 2. Combining terms gives 3m + 10.

6. Factor completely: 12p² + 18p

Answer: B) 6p(2p + 3)
The GCF is 6p. Dividing the terms by 6p gives 2p and 3.

7. Which is equivalent to 2(3x - 5) + x?

Answer: A) 7x - 10
Distribute: 6x - 10 + x. Combine the x-terms to obtain 7x - 10.

8. Simplify: -(2t - 7) + 3t

Answer: C) t + 7
The leading negative changes both signs: -2t + 7 + 3t = t + 7.

9. Factor completely: 21ab - 14a

Answer: A) 7a(3b - 2)
Both terms share 7a. The quotients are 3b and -2, so distribution restores the original expression.

10. Simplify: 0.5(8r + 6) - r

Answer: A) 3r + 3
Half of 8r + 6 is 4r + 3. Then 4r + 3 - r = 3r + 3.

11. Simplify: (3/4)(8k - 12) + 2k

Answer: B) 8k - 9
Distribute 3/4: 6k - 9 + 2k. Combining like terms gives 8k - 9.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. Which expression is equivalent to 6(x + 2) - 4x?

Answer: C) 2x + 12
Expand to 6x + 12 - 4x, then combine like terms: 2x + 12.

13. A rectangle has width 3x and length x + 5. Which expanded expression represents its area?

Answer: A) 3x² + 15x
Area = width × length = 3x(x + 5) = 3x² + 15x.

14. Which factored form is equivalent to 18q² - 24q?

Answer: B) 6q(3q - 4)
The greatest common factor is 6q. Factoring it out gives 6q(3q - 4).

15. For all x, 4(2x + 1) + c is equivalent to 8x + 11. What is c?

Answer: B) 7
Expand the left side: 8x + 4 + c. Matching constants with 8x + 11 gives 4 + c = 11, so c = 7.

16. A student rewrites -3(2y - 5) + y as -5y + 15. Which statement is correct?

Answer: A) The rewrite is correct.
Distribute -3: -6y + 15 + y. Combining like terms gives -5y + 15, exactly as stated.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. Simplify completely: 2[3x - 4(x - 5)] - (x + 1)

Answer: A) -3x + 39
Inside the brackets, 3x - 4x + 20 = -x + 20. Then 2(-x + 20) - x - 1 = -2x + 40 - x - 1 = -3x + 39.

18. Which expression is equivalent to (1/2)(6z - 8) - 3(z - 5)?

Answer: A) 11
Expand both parts: 3z - 4 - 3z + 15. The z-terms cancel and the constants combine to 11.
