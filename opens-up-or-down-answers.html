Opens Up or Down

SKILL CHECK

1. A — Up. a=4>0.

2. B — Down. a=−2<0.

3. A — a. The leading coefficient of x² controls the opening direction.

CORE PRACTICE

4. A — Up. The implied coefficient is a=1>0.

5. B — Down. a=−1<0.

6. A — Up. a=0.25>0.

7. B — Down. a=−3/5<0.

8. A — Minimum. a=9>0, so the parabola opens upward and its vertex is a minimum.

9. B — Maximum. a=−7<0, so the parabola opens downward and its vertex is a maximum.

10. B — Down. 2x²+y=5 gives y=−2x²+5, so a=−2.

11. B — Down. y+3x²=6x−1 gives y=−3x²+6x−1, so a=−3.

EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. A — k<0. Downward opening requires the leading coefficient to be negative.

13. A — y=3x²−8x+1. A minimum requires a>0; only choice A has positive a.

14. A — m>4. Upward opening requires m−4>0, hence m>4.

15. A — p>2. A maximum requires 2−p<0, so p>2.

16. A — q>0. Rearrange to y=−qx²+4x+1. Downward means −q<0, therefore q>0.

CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. A — −3<r<3. Downward requires r²−9<0. Thus r²<9, so |r|<3.

18. A — t<−5 or t>1. Upward requires (t−1)(t+5)>0. A product of these factors is positive outside the roots −5 and 1.
