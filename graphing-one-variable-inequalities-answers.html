ANSWERS & SOLUTIONS

Graphing One-Variable Inequalities

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. x < 4

Answer: A) Open circle at 4, shade left
The strict symbol < excludes 4, so use an open circle. Values less than 4 lie to the left.

2. x ≥ −2

Answer: B) Closed circle at −2, shade right
The symbol ≥ includes −2, so use a closed circle. Greater values lie to the right.

3. Open at 7, shaded right

Answer: C) x > 7
Open excludes 7; shading right means values greater than 7.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. x ≤ 3

Answer: A) Closed circle at 3, shade left
≤ includes 3 and represents values to its left.

5. x > −5

Answer: B) Open circle at −5, shade right
The endpoint is excluded and all greater values are to the right.

6. Closed at 1, shaded left

Answer: B) x ≤ 1
Closed includes 1; left means values less than 1.

7. Open at −4, shaded left

Answer: A) x < −4
Open excludes −4; left means smaller values.

8. Included in x ≥ 2

Answer: C) 2
Because ≥ includes the endpoint, x = 2 satisfies the inequality.

9. Included in x < 0

Answer: C) −3
−3 is negative, so −3 < 0. The other choices are zero or positive.

10. Solve 2x + 3 ≤ 11

Answer: B) Closed circle at 4, shade left
2x + 3 ≤ 11 ⇒ 2x ≤ 8 ⇒ x ≤ 4. The endpoint 4 is included.

11. Solve −3x > 12

Answer: A) Open circle at −4, shade left
Divide by −3 and reverse the inequality: x < −4. The strict inequality uses an open circle.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. Temperature at most 18°C

Answer: A) Closed circle at 18, shade left
“At most 18” means t ≤ 18, so 18 is included and lower temperatures are allowed.

13. Score must exceed 70

Answer: B) Open circle at 70, shade right
“Exceed 70” means s > 70, excluding 70 and including greater scores.

14. Balance at least $250

Answer: B) Closed circle at 250, shade right
“At least” means b ≥ 250, so 250 is included.

15. Lift carries no more than 600 kg

Answer: A) Closed circle at 600, shade left
“No more than” means w ≤ 600, including the 600 kg limit.

16. Task completed in less than 45 minutes

Answer: B) Open circle at 45, shade left
“Less than 45” means t < 45. The endpoint is excluded.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. Solve −2(3x − 1) ≥ 10

Answer: A) Closed circle at −4/3, shade left
Expand: −6x + 2 ≥ 10 ⇒ −6x ≥ 8. Divide by −6 and reverse: x ≤ −8/6 = −4/3. Equality is included.

18. Closed endpoint at −1.5; includes 4 but not −2

Answer: C) x ≥ −1.5
Including 4 means the graph extends right from −1.5. A closed endpoint includes −1.5, so x ≥ −1.5; −2 is correctly excluded.
