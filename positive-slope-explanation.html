Positive Slope
Algebra
01 · LESSON OBJECTIVE

Recognize and calculate positive slope.

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to identify a line with positive slope, calculate its slope from points, tables, graphs, or equations, and explain what a positive slope means in context.

x increases→y increases
02 · WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

A positive slope rises from left to right.

For a line with positive slope, larger x-values correspond to larger y-values. In the slope formula, the changes in x and y have the same sign, so their quotient is positive.

Graphrises rightThe line goes upward as x increases.
Formulam > 0The slope value is positive.
Tabley increasesAs x increases, y also increases at a constant rate.
Equationy = mx + bThe coefficient m is positive.
03 · KEY DEFINITIONS

Positive Slope

A slope greater than zero.

m > 0

Rate of Change

The change in y for each unit change in x.

Δy / Δx

Increasing Linear Function

A linear function whose output increases as its input increases.

f(x₂) > f(x₁) when x₂ > x₁
04 · CORE RULE / FORMULA

Positive slope means the numerator and denominator have the same sign.

m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁) > 0

If you move from the left point to the right point, both Δx and Δy are positive. If you reverse both points, both changes are negative. Either way, the quotient stays positive.

05 · HOW IT WORKS

Check the sign, then compute the rate.

06 · WORKED EXAMPLE 1 — BASIC

Find the slope through (1, 3) and (5, 11).

Δy = 11 − 3 = 8Δx = 5 − 1 = 4m = 8/4 = 2
MeaningFor every 1-unit increase in x, y increases by 2 units.
07 · WORKED EXAMPLE 2 — FRACTIONAL SLOPE

Find the slope through (−2, 1) and (4, 4).

Δy = 4 − 1 = 3Δx = 4 − (−2) = 6m = 3/6 = 1/2

Visual meaning: A positive slope less than 1 rises more slowly than it runs.

08 · WORKED EXAMPLE 3 — FROM AN EQUATION

What is the slope of y = 3.5x − 8?

y = mx + bm = 3.5
ConclusionBecause 3.5 > 0, the line has positive slope.
09 · WORKED EXAMPLE 4 — FROM A TABLE

A table contains (0, 7), (2, 13), and (4, 19). Find the slope.

Δy = 13 − 7 = 6Δx = 2 − 0 = 2m = 6/2 = 3

The second interval gives the same rate: (19 − 13)/(4 − 2) = 6/2 = 3.

10 · WORKED EXAMPLE 5 — REVERSED POINT ORDER

Use (6, 9) and (2, 1) to find the slope.

Δy = 1 − 9 = −8Δx = 2 − 6 = −4m = (−8)/(−4) = 2

Key idea: Reversing both subtraction orders does not change the slope.

11 · WORKED EXAMPLE 6 — EXAM STYLE

A tank contains 18 liters of water after 2 minutes and 42 liters after 8 minutes. The amount changes linearly. Find and interpret the slope.

Δvolume = 42 − 18 = 24 LΔtime = 8 − 2 = 6 minm = 24/6 = 4 L/min
InterpretationThe amount of water increases by 4 liters each minute.

Enter

Look for

Use it to answer

Why it works

In y = mx + b, the coefficient of x is the slope. Here m = 2, so the function increases as x increases.

Faster or not?

Reading a visible slope or the coefficient m is usually faster by hand. Desmos is useful for visual confirmation and for checking a graph-based question.

Math cross-check: 2(0)+1=1, 2(1)+1=3, and 2(2)+1=5, so all three verified points lie on the line.

12 · COMMON MISTAKES
Judging by steepness only

Steepness tells magnitude, not sign. A positive line must rise from left to right.

Reversing only one subtraction

Use the same point order for both y and x differences.

Assuming every increasing-looking data set is linear

For a linear relationship, the rate of change must stay constant.

Ignoring units

In context, slope units are output units per input unit.

13 · EXAM STRATEGY

Predict the sign before you calculate.

First decide whether the relationship rises or falls as x increases. If it rises, your computed slope should be positive. This quick sign check catches many subtraction errors.

15 · LESSON RECAP

Positive slope describes an increasing linear relationship.

  1. A positive-slope line rises from left to right.
  2. Its slope satisfies m > 0.
  3. In y = mx + b, a positive coefficient of x gives positive slope.
  4. From points, compute m = Δy/Δx using consistent subtraction order.
  5. In context, interpret the positive rate with correct units.
