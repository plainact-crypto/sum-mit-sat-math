Sum of Roots
Advanced Math
01 · LESSON OBJECTIVE

Find the sum of a quadratic’s roots without solving for each root.

For a quadratic in standard form, the coefficients tell you the sum of the roots directly. This is especially useful when a question asks only for the sum, not the individual solutions.

02 · CORE RULE

For ax²+bx+c=0, the sum of the roots is −b/a.

r₁+r₂=−b/aa≠0

The rule follows from expanding a(x−r₁)(x−r₂). The coefficient of x is −a(r₁+r₂), so matching coefficients gives b=−a(r₁+r₂).

03 · WORKED EXAMPLE 1

Find the sum of the roots of x²−7x+10=0.

a=1, b=−7r₁+r₂=−(−7)/1=7
Math cross-checkx²−7x+10=(x−2)(x−5), so the roots are 2 and 5, and 2+5=7.
04 · WORKED EXAMPLE 2

Find the sum of the roots of 3x²+12x−8=0.

a=3, b=12r₁+r₂=−12/3=−4

You do not need to compute the roots. The coefficient rule gives the requested quantity immediately.

05 · WORKED EXAMPLE 3

A quadratic 2x²+kx+15=0 has roots whose sum is 6. Find k.

6=−k/2k=−12
Math cross-checkWith k=−12, the quadratic is 2x²−12x+15=0, and −b/a=−(−12)/2=6.

Why it worksRoots are x-values where the quadratic equals zero.Desmos shows those zeros as x-intercepts, so their x-coordinates can verify the root sum.
Faster or not?No, not when the question asks only for the sum.The coefficient rule −b/a is faster and exact. Desmos is useful as a check when the roots are easy to read.
Math cross-checkAt x=2, 2²−7(2)+10=4−14+10=0. At x=5, 5²−7(5)+10=25−35+10=0. Their sum is 7, matching −b/a=−(−7)/1=7.
06 · COMMON MISTAKES
Forgetting the negative sign

The sum is −b/a, not b/a.

Using c instead of b

The sum uses the x-coefficient b. The constant term is used in the product-of-roots rule.

Reading coefficients before standard form

First write the equation as ax²+bx+c=0, then identify a and b.

07 · EXAM STRATEGY

If only the sum is requested, do not solve the quadratic.

Put the equation in standard form, identify a and b, and calculate −b/a. Solve for individual roots only when the question actually needs them.

09 · LESSON RECAP
  1. Write the quadratic as ax²+bx+c=0.
  2. The sum of the roots is −b/a.
  3. Keep the negative sign attached to b.
  4. Use the rule directly when individual roots are not requested.
  5. Verify with factoring, substitution, or graph zeros when useful.
