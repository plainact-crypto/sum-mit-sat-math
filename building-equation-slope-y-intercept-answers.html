ANSWERS & SOLUTIONS

Building an Equation from Slope and Y-Intercept

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. A line has slope 2 and y-intercept 5.

Answer: B) y = 2x + 5
Use y = mx + b with m = 2 and b = 5. Substitution gives y = 2x + 5.

2. A line has slope −3 and y-intercept 4.

Answer: A) y = −3x + 4
Here m = −3 and b = 4, so y = −3x + 4.

3. A line has slope 1/2 and y-intercept −6.

Answer: C) y = (1/2)x − 6
Substitute m = 1/2 and b = −6 into y = mx + b.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. A line has slope 0 and y-intercept 9.

Answer: A) y = 9
y = 0x + 9 simplifies to y = 9.

5. A line has slope −5 and y-intercept −2.

Answer: C) y = −5x − 2
With m = −5 and b = −2, the equation is y = −5x − 2.

6. A quantity starts at 30 and increases by 7 for each 1-unit increase in x.

Answer: B) y = 7x + 30
The initial value is b = 30 and the rate of change is m = 7, so y = 7x + 30.

7. A quantity starts at 18 and decreases by 4 for each 1-unit increase in x.

Answer: B) y = −4x + 18
The initial value is 18 and the rate is −4, giving y = −4x + 18.

8. A line has slope −2/3 and y-intercept 5.

Answer: C) y = −(2/3)x + 5
Use m = −2/3 and b = 5 in y = mx + b.

9. A line has slope 6 and crosses the y-axis at (0, −8).

Answer: A) y = 6x − 8
The point (0,−8) gives b = −8. With slope 6, y = 6x − 8.

10. A line has slope 1 and y-intercept −12.

Answer: A) y = x − 12
Since m = 1, mx = x. With b = −12, y = x − 12.

11. A line has slope −0.5 and y-intercept 3.5.

Answer: C) y = −0.5x + 3.5
Substitute m = −0.5 and b = 3.5 directly into slope-intercept form.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. A service charges an initial fee of $25 plus $8 per month.

Answer: B) C = 8m + 25
The monthly rate is 8, so the variable term is 8m. The initial fee 25 is the intercept.

13. A tank contains 140 liters at the start and loses 6 liters each minute.

Answer: B) V = −6t + 140
The initial volume is 140 liters and the rate of change is −6 liters per minute.

14. A line has y-intercept −7. For every increase of 2 in x, y increases by 3.

Answer: B) y = (3/2)x − 7
Slope is rise/run = 3/2. With b = −7, y = (3/2)x − 7.

15. A linear function has y-intercept 11. Whenever x increases by 4, y decreases by 10.

Answer: C) y = −(5/2)x + 11
Slope = −10/4 = −5/2. Using b = 11 gives y = −(5/2)x + 11.

16. Which equation has slope 4/3 and y-intercept −5?

Answer: A) y = (4/3)x − 5
The coefficient of x is 4/3 and the constant term is −5, exactly matching y = mx + b.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. A line has slope a − 1 and y-intercept 2a + 3. If its slope is 4, which equation represents the line?

Answer: B) y = 4x + 13
Set a − 1 = 4, so a = 5. Then b = 2(5) + 3 = 13. Therefore y = 4x + 13. Check: the coefficient of x is 4 and the y-intercept is 13.

18. A line crosses the y-axis at (0, 12). When x increases by 5, y decreases by 15.

Answer: A) y = −3x + 12
The slope is Δy/Δx = −15/5 = −3. The y-intercept is 12, so y = −3x + 12.
