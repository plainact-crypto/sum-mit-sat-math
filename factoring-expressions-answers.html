Factoring Expressions

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. Factor completely: 8x + 12

Answer: A) 4(2x + 3)
The GCF of 8 and 12 is 4. Dividing each term by 4 gives 2x + 3; distributing 4 returns 8x + 12.

2. Factor completely: 15y - 25

Answer: A) 5(3y - 5)
The GCF is 5. Since 15y ÷ 5 = 3y and -25 ÷ 5 = -5, the complete factorization is 5(3y - 5).

3. Factor completely: 18a + 24

Answer: A) 6(3a + 4)
The greatest common factor is 6. Dividing term by term gives 3a + 4.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. Factor completely: 12x² + 18x

Answer: A) 6x(2x + 3)
Both terms share 6x: 12x² ÷ 6x = 2x and 18x ÷ 6x = 3.

5. Factor completely: 20m³ - 15m²

Answer: A) 5m²(4m - 3)
The numerical GCF is 5 and the smallest shared power is m², so the GCF is 5m².

6. Factor completely: 14ab + 21a

Answer: A) 7a(2b + 3)
The terms share 7a. Dividing gives 2b and 3.

7. Factor completely: 16p²q + 24pq²

Answer: A) 8pq(2p + 3q)
The coefficient GCF is 8, and both terms contain p and q. Factoring 8pq leaves 2p + 3q.

8. Factor completely: 9x²y - 6xy² + 3xy

Answer: A) 3xy(3x - 2y + 1)
All three terms share 3xy. The quotients are 3x, -2y, and 1.

9. Factor with a negative GCF: -10t² + 25t

Answer: A) -5t(2t - 5)
Factoring -5t makes the first term inside positive: (-10t²)/(-5t)=2t and 25t/(-5t)=-5.

10. Complete GCF factorization of 24r³s² + 36r²s³

Answer: A) 12r²s²(2r + 3s)
gcd(24,36)=12; the minimum shared powers are r² and s². The remaining terms are 2r and 3s.

11. Factor completely: 35u²v - 49uv²

Answer: A) 7uv(5u - 7v)
The GCF is 7uv. Division leaves 5u - 7v.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. Factor completely: 18x + 30

Answer: A) 6(3x + 5)
The GCF of 18 and 30 is 6. The inside coefficients 3 and 5 have no further common factor.

13. Rectangle area 12x² + 20x with one side 4x

Answer: A) 3x + 5
Divide area by the known side: (12x² + 20x)/(4x) = 3x + 5. Multiplying 4x(3x + 5) restores the area.

14. Factor completely: 27a³b² - 18a²b³

Answer: A) 9a²b²(3a - 2b)
The coefficient GCF is 9, with shared a²b². The quotients are 3a and -2b.

15. GCF form of 32x⁴ + 48x³

Answer: A) 16x³(2x + 3)
gcd(32,48)=16 and the minimum x-power is x³. Distribution verifies 32x⁴ + 48x³.

16. Total 24a + 36b using the greatest common numerical factor

Answer: A) 12(2a + 3b)
The greatest common numerical factor of 24 and 36 is 12, so 24a + 36b = 12(2a + 3b).
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. Factor completely: -42x³y² + 56x²y³ - 14x²y²

Answer: A) -14x²y²(3x - 4y + 1)
Using negative GCF -14x²y² gives quotients 3x, -4y, and 1. Expanding gives -42x³y² + 56x²y³ - 14x²y².

18. Factor completely: 45m⁴n³ - 30m³n⁴ + 75m³n³

Answer: A) 15m³n³(3m - 2n + 5)
The coefficient GCF is 15 and the minimum shared powers are m³n³. The quotients are 3m, -2n, and 5.
