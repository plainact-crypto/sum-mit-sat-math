Combining Like Terms

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. Simplify 4x + 3x

Answer: A) 7x
Both terms have the same variable part x, so add the coefficients: 4+3=7.

2. Simplify 9a - 5a + 2

Answer: A) 4a + 2
Combine the a-terms: 9a-5a=4a. The constant 2 is not like an a-term, so it remains.

3. Simplify 6m + 4 - 2m - 7

Answer: A) 4m - 3
6m-2m=4m and 4-7=-3, giving 4m-3.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. Simplify 3x + 2y + 5x - y

Answer: A) 8x + y
3x+5x=8x and 2y-y=y.

5. Simplify 7p - 3 + 2 - 4p

Answer: A) 3p - 1
7p-4p=3p and -3+2=-1.

6. Simplify 5x² + 2x - 3x² + 7x

Answer: A) 2x² + 9x
The x²-terms combine to (5-3)x²=2x², while the x-terms combine to (2+7)x=9x. Terms with x² and x are not like terms.

7. Simplify -4a + 9a - 6

Answer: A) 5a - 6
-4a+9a=5a; the constant -6 remains.

8. Simplify 2m + 3n - 5m + 4n

Answer: A) -3m + 7n
2m-5m=-3m and 3n+4n=7n.

9. Simplify (1/2)x + (3/4)x

Answer: A) (5/4)x
Use a common denominator: 1/2=2/4, so 2/4+3/4=5/4.

10. Simplify 0.6y - 1.4y + 2

Answer: A) -0.8y + 2
0.6-1.4=-0.8, so the y-terms become -0.8y; the constant stays 2.

11. Simplify -x + 5 + 6x - 9

Answer: A) 5x - 4
-x+6x=5x and 5-9=-4.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. Rectangle perimeter

Answer: A) 8x + 2
Perimeter is 2L+2W. Substitute L=3x+2 and W=x-1: 2(3x+2)+2(x-1)=6x+4+2x-2=8x+2.

13. Find the missing coefficient k

Answer: C) 5
4t+7+kt-2=(4+k)t+5. Matching 9t+5 gives 4+k=9, so k=5.

14. Find a from the simplified expression

Answer: D) 7
2x²+3x²=5x². The x-terms are ax-5x=(a-5)x. Matching 5x²+2x gives a-5=2, so a=7.

15. Simplify 3p + 2q - 5 + p - 4q + 9

Answer: A) 4p - 2q + 4
3p+p=4p, 2q-4q=-2q, and -5+9=4.

16. Equivalent expression

Answer: A) 9x + 5
6x+3x=9x and -2+7=5, so the expression is 9x+5.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. Simplify 2x + 3y + 4x - 5y + x

Answer: A) 7x - 2y
2x+4x+x=7x and 3y-5y=-2y.

18. Simplify 0.25a + (3/2)a - 0.75a + 4

Answer: A) a + 4
Since 3/2=1.5, the coefficient of a is 0.25+1.5-0.75=1. Therefore the expression is a+4.
