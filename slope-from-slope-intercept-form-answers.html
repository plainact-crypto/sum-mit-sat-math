Slope from Slope-Intercept Form

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. What is the slope of y = 5x − 2?

Answer: C) 5
In slope-intercept form y = mx + b, the slope is the coefficient m of x. Here m = 5.

2. What is the slope of y = −3x + 7?

Answer: B) −3
The coefficient of x is −3, so the slope is −3.

3. What is the slope of y = (2/5)x + 9?

Answer: A) 2/5
The coefficient multiplying x is 2/5, so m = 2/5.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. What is the slope of y = x − 11?

Answer: D) 1
The x-term is x = 1x, so its coefficient and the slope are both 1.

5. What is the slope of y = −x + 4?

Answer: B) −1
Because −x means −1x, the slope is −1.

6. What is the slope of y = 0.75x − 6?

Answer: C) 0.75
The coefficient of x is 0.75, so m = 0.75.

7. What is the slope of y = −1.2x + 8?

Answer: B) −1.2
The x-coefficient is −1.2, so the slope is −1.2.

8. What is the slope of y = (7/3)x − 5?

Answer: C) 7/3
The slope is the coefficient of x, which is 7/3.

9. What is the slope of y = 9x?

Answer: C) 9
This can be written as y = 9x + 0, so m = 9.

10. What is the slope of y = −(5/8)x − 3?

Answer: A) −5/8
The coefficient of x is −5/8, so the slope is −5/8.

11. What is the slope of y = 0x + 14?

Answer: A) 0
The coefficient of x is 0. The equation simplifies to y = 14, a horizontal line, so its slope is 0.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. A delivery company models total cost C, in dollars, for x miles by C = 2.4x + 6. What does the slope represent?

Answer: B) A $2.40 increase per mile
The slope is 2.4 dollars per mile. Each additional mile increases the modeled total cost by $2.40.

13. The amount W, in liters, of water in a tank after t minutes is W = −6t + 180. What does the slope represent?

Answer: C) The amount decreases by 6 liters per minute
The slope is −6 liters per minute. The negative sign means the amount of water decreases, at a rate of 6 liters each minute.

14. A streaming plan’s monthly total y, in dollars, after x premium rentals is y = 3.75x + 12. What is the slope, including units?

Answer: B) $3.75 per rental
The coefficient of x is 3.75, and x counts rentals, so the slope is $3.75 per rental.

15. A cyclist’s distance y, in miles, after x hours is modeled by y = (9/2)x + 1. What does the slope mean?

Answer: C) The distance increases by 9/2 miles per hour
The slope is 9/2 miles per hour, so each additional hour increases the modeled distance by 9/2 miles.

16. The value y of a quantity is modeled by y = −0.25x + 40. If x increases by 4, how does y change?

Answer: B) It decreases by 1
Change in y = slope × change in x = (−0.25)(4) = −1. Therefore y decreases by 1.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. The line y = (k − 2)x + 5 has slope 6. What is k?

Answer: C) 8
The coefficient of x equals the slope, so k − 2 = 6. Add 2 to both sides: k = 8.

18. The line y = (3p + 1)x − 4 has slope −8. What is p?

Answer: A) −3
Set the x-coefficient equal to the slope: 3p + 1 = −8. Then 3p = −9, so p = −3.
