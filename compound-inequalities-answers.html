Compound Inequalities

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. Solve 2 < x + 1 ≤ 6.

Answer: A) 1 < x ≤ 5
Subtract 1 from all three parts: 1<x≤5.

2. Interpret x < −3 or x ≥ 4.

Answer: B) Values below −3 together with values at least 4
OR combines the two separate solution regions: x is below −3, or it is 4 or greater.

3. Solve −2 ≤ 3x + 1 < 10.

Answer: B) −1 ≤ x < 3
Subtract 1: −3≤3x<9. Divide all parts by positive 3: −1≤x<3.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. Solve 5 < 2x + 1 ≤ 13.

Answer: A) 2 < x ≤ 6
Subtract 1: 4<2x≤12. Divide by 2: 2<x≤6.

5. Solve −7 ≤ 2x − 1 < 9.

Answer: B) −3 ≤ x < 5
Add 1: −6≤2x<10. Divide by 2: −3≤x<5.

6. Solve both 3x − 2 < 7 and x + 4 ≥ 2.

Answer: A) −2 ≤ x < 3
The first inequality gives x<3; the second gives x≥−2. Their intersection is −2≤x<3.

7. Solve 2x + 5 ≤ 1 or 3x − 6 > 9.

Answer: B) x ≤ −2 or x > 5
Solve separately: 2x≤−4 gives x≤−2, while 3x>15 gives x>5. Keep both regions because the connector is OR.

8. Solve −4 < −2x ≤ 8.

Answer: A) −4 ≤ x < 2
Divide all parts by −2 and reverse both inequality signs: 2>x≥−4, equivalent to −4≤x<2.

9. Solve 1 ≤ (x − 2)/3 < 4.

Answer: B) 5 ≤ x < 14
Multiply by 3: 3≤x−2<12. Add 2: 5≤x<14.

10. Solve both 4x − 3 > 9 and 2x + 1 ≤ 11.

Answer: A) 3 < x ≤ 5
The inequalities give x>3 and x≤5. Their common region is 3<x≤5.

11. Solve x/2 − 1 ≤ 2 or 5 − x < 1.

Answer: D) All real numbers
The first gives x≤6. The second gives −x<−4, so x>4. Their OR-union covers every real number.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. Training score target band

Answer: A) 4 ≤ n < 8
Write 70≤50+5n<90. Subtract 50 and divide by 5: 4≤n<8.

13. Workbook spending range

Answer: A) 5 ≤ b < 10
The condition is 20<100−8b≤60. From 20<100−8b, b<10; from 100−8b≤60, b≥5. Together: 5≤b<10.

14. Platform crate limit

Answer: B) 6 ≤ c ≤ 14
Model 300≤150+25c≤500. Subtract 150: 150≤25c≤350. Divide by 25: 6≤c≤14.

15. Contest advancing ratings

Answer: B) r ≤ 12 or r ≥ 18
“At most 12” means r≤12, while “at least 18” means r≥18. The rule uses OR.

16. Sensor alert readings

Answer: C) t < −2 or t > 6
“Below −2” and “above 6” are strict inequalities, joined by OR.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. Solve −3 < 2 − 5x ≤ 12.

Answer: A) −2 ≤ x < 1
Subtract 2: −5<−5x≤10. Divide by −5 and reverse both signs: 1>x≥−2, so −2≤x<1.

18. Solve both 2(3x − 1) ≤ 4x + 14 and −3(x − 5) < 6.

Answer: A) 3 < x ≤ 8
First: 6x−2≤4x+14 gives x≤8. Second: −3x+15<6 gives −3x<−9, so x>3 after dividing by −3 and reversing. Together: 3<x≤8.
