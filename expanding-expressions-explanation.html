Expanding Expressions
Advanced Math
01 · LESSON OBJECTIVE

Remove grouping symbols and simplify without changing the expression's value.

Expanding an expression means using the distributive property wherever grouping symbols appear, then combining like terms. The goal is an equivalent expression with no unnecessary parentheses.

3(x + 4) - 2(x - 5)→x + 22
02 · CORE IDEA

Distribute first. Combine second.

a(b + c) = ab + ac

Order matters: distribute every outside factor through its entire group before combining terms. Keep each sign attached to the term it belongs to.

03 · WHAT COUNTS AS EXPANDED?

Grouped form

An expression still contains a factor multiplying parentheses or brackets.

2(x + 5) + 3(x - 1)

Expanded form

All required distribution has been carried out.

2x + 10 + 3x - 3

Simplified expanded form

After expansion, like terms are combined.

5x + 7
04 · WORKED EXAMPLE 1 — TWO GROUPS

Expand and simplify: 3(x + 4) - 2(x - 5)

3x + 12 - 2x + 10(3x - 2x) + (12 + 10)x + 22

The second group is multiplied by -2, so both products must reflect that factor.

Math cross-checkAt x=5: the original is 3(9)-2(0)=27, and x+22=27.
05 · WORKED EXAMPLE 2 — NEGATIVE SIGN BEFORE A GROUP

Expand and simplify: -(3a - 4) + 2(a + 5)

-3a + 4 + 2a + 10-a + 14

A minus sign before parentheses acts like multiplication by -1. Every term inside changes sign.

Math cross-checkAt a=1: the original is -(3-4)+2(6)=13, and -1+14=13.
06 · WORKED EXAMPLE 3 — FRACTION FACTOR

Expand and simplify: 1/2(8p - 6) + 3(p + 2)

4p - 3 + 3p + 67p + 3

Fraction factors follow the same rule: multiply the factor by every term in the group.

Math cross-checkAt p=4: both forms equal 31.
07 · WORKED EXAMPLE 4 — NESTED GROUPING

Expand and simplify: 3[2x - (x - 4)]

3[2x - x + 4]3[x + 4]3x + 12

Work from the inner grouping outward. The subtraction before (x-4) changes both signs inside that group.

Math cross-checkAt x=2: the original is 3[4-(-2)]=18, and 3(2)+12=18.
08 · A RELIABLE EXPANSION METHOD
  1. Find every factor immediately outside a pair of grouping symbols.
  2. Distribute that factor to every term inside the group.
  3. If a group is preceded by only a minus sign, treat it as a factor of -1.
  4. Remove the grouping symbols after all required products are written.
  5. Combine only terms with the same variable part and exponent.
  6. Scan once more for any grouping that still needs expansion.
09 · SIGNS THAT CAUSE ERRORS
Positive factor2(x - 3)2x - 6
Negative factor-2(x - 3)-2x + 6
Minus before group-(x + 7)-x - 7
Minus before group-(x - 7)-x + 7
10 · COMMON MISTAKES
Distributing to only one term

4(x+3) becomes 4x+12, not 4x+3.

Dropping the sign of the outside factor

In -3(x-2), the factor is -3, not 3.

Combining unlike terms

4x+3x² cannot become 7x². The variable parts are different.

Stopping too early

2x+10+3x-3 is expanded, but 5x+7 is the simplified expanded form.

11 · EXAM STRATEGY

Write one clean distribution line before combining.

For a test-style expression with several groups, resist doing everything mentally. One intermediate line showing every distributed product catches most sign errors. Then combine like terms in a separate pass.

2(x + 5) + 3(x - 1)→2x+10+3x-3 → 5x+7

13 · LESSON RECAP
  1. Expanding removes grouping by distribution.
  2. The outside factor multiplies every term inside its group.
  3. A minus sign before a group is a factor of -1.
  4. For nested grouping, simplify from the inside outward.
  5. After expanding, combine like terms to finish the simplification.
  6. Substitution can verify that the original and expanded forms are equivalent.
