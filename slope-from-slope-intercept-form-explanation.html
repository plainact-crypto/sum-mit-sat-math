Slope from Slope-Intercept Form
Algebra
01 · LESSON OBJECTIVE

Read the slope directly from a linear equation in slope-intercept form.

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to identify the slope of a line written as y = mx + b, including positive, negative, fractional, decimal, and implied slopes.

y = 3x + 2→slope = 3
02 · WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

In slope-intercept form, the coefficient of x is the slope.

The equation y = mx + b has two key numbers. The coefficient m tells you the slope, and b tells you the y-intercept. For this lesson, focus only on reading m.

Formy = mx + bThe standard slope-intercept pattern.
SlopemThe coefficient multiplying x.
Y-interceptbThe constant term; useful, but not the target skill here.
Meaning of sloperise / runChange in y for each 1-unit change in x.
03 · KEY DEFINITIONS

Slope

The rate at which y changes as x changes.

m = Δy / Δx

Coefficient

A number multiplying a variable.

−4x → coefficient = −4

Slope-Intercept Form

A linear equation written with y isolated and x in the first-degree term.

y = mx + b
04 · CORE RULE / FORMULA

If the equation is already y = mx + b, the slope is m.

y = mx + b → slope = m
05 · HOW IT WORKS

Use a quick three-step read

01

02

03

06 · WORKED EXAMPLE 1 — BASIC

Find the slope of y = 3x + 2.

  1. Compare with y = mx + b.The x-term is 3x.
  2. Read the coefficient of x.m = 3
  3. State the slope.slope = 3
InterpretationFor every increase of 1 in x, y increases by 3.
07 · WORKED EXAMPLE 2 — NEGATIVE FRACTION

Find the slope of y = −(1/2)x + 7.

y = mx + bm = −1/2slope = −1/2

Remember: The negative sign is part of the slope. A negative slope means the line decreases from left to right.

08 · WORKED EXAMPLE 3 — IMPLIED COEFFICIENT

Find the slope of y = x − 4.

x = 1xy = 1x − 4slope = 1

When no number is written in front of x, the coefficient is 1.

09 · WORKED EXAMPLE 4 — IMPLIED NEGATIVE ONE

Find the slope of y = −x + 9.

−x = −1xy = −1x + 9slope = −1
Sign checkThe line falls 1 unit in y for each 1-unit increase in x.
10 · WORKED EXAMPLE 5 — EXAM STYLE

A model for the total cost y, in dollars, of renting equipment for x hours is y = 2.5x + 18. What does the slope represent?

y = 2.5x + 18m = 2.5
Exam connection

The slope 2.5 means the total cost increases by $2.50 for each additional hour of rental. In school and exam questions, slope is often interpreted as a rate with units.

VISUAL GRAPH

Connect the coefficient of x to the line's rise.

For y = 3x − 4, the coefficient of x is 3. The graph shows that each 1-unit increase in x raises y by 3.

Math cross-checkSubstitution verifies three points: x = 0 gives y = −4, x = 1 gives y = −1, and x = 2 gives y = 2. From (0,−4) to (1,−1), rise/run = 3/1 = 3, matching the x-coefficient.
DESMOS STRATEGY

Use a graphing tool to verify the slope you read from the equation.

Enter

y = 3x - 4

Look for

A line crossing the y-axis at −4 and rising 3 units for every 1 unit moved to the right.

Use it to answer

Confirm that the visual rise/run is 3/1, so the slope is 3.

Why it works

In y = mx + b, the coefficient m is the constant rate of change, so the graph must rise or fall according to that value.

Faster or not?

Reading m directly is faster when the equation is already in slope-intercept form. Desmos is useful as a visual verification step.

Math cross-check: The verified points (0,−4), (1,−1), and (2,2) all satisfy y = 3x − 4, and each 1-unit increase in x changes y by 3.

11 · COMMON MISTAKES
Using the constant term as the slope

In y = 4x + 7, the slope is 4, not 7.

Dropping the negative sign

In y = −3x + 5, the slope is −3.

Missing an implied 1

In y = x + 6, the slope is 1.

Missing an implied −1

In y = −x − 2, the slope is −1.

12 · EXAM STRATEGY

When the equation is already in slope-intercept form, do not overwork it.

Look for y = mx + b and read the coefficient of x. If the question asks what the slope means, attach the correct units: change in the output for each one-unit increase in the input.

y = −0.8x + 12→m = −0.8

14 · LESSON RECAP

The x-coefficient is the slope.

  1. Slope-intercept form is y = mx + b.
  2. The coefficient m is the slope.
  3. Keep the sign attached to the coefficient.
  4. If x has no written coefficient, use 1; if the term is −x, use −1.
  5. In context, interpret slope as change in y per 1 unit of x.
y = mx + bslope = mm = Δy / Δx
