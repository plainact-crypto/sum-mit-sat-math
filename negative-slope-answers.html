Negative Slope

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. A line passes through (1, 10) and (5, 2). What is its slope?

Answer: B) −2
m = (2 − 10)/(5 − 1) = −8/4 = −2.

2. What is the slope of y = −0.6x + 7?

Answer: B) −0.6
In y = mx + b, the coefficient of x is the slope, so m = −0.6.

3. A table contains the points (−3, 8), (1, 0), and (5, −8). What is the slope?

Answer: B) −2
Using (−3,8) and (1,0), m = (0 − 8)/(1 − (−3)) = −8/4 = −2. The next pair gives the same rate.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. Find the slope through (−4, 9) and (2, −3).

Answer: B) −2
m = (−3 − 9)/(2 − (−4)) = −12/6 = −2.

5. Find the slope through (7, −1) and (3, 9).

Answer: A) −5/2
m = (9 − (−1))/(3 − 7) = 10/−4 = −5/2.

6. What is the slope of y = −(7/3)x + 4?

Answer: A) −7/3
The coefficient of x is the slope, so m = −7/3.

7. A line falls 18 units while moving 6 units to the right. What is its slope?

Answer: B) −3
Slope = rise/run = −18/6 = −3.

8. A table includes the points (2, 11) and (6, 3). What is the slope?

Answer: B) −2
m = (3 − 11)/(6 − 2) = −8/4 = −2.

9. For a line, Δx = −5 and Δy = 20. What is the slope?

Answer: A) −4
m = Δy/Δx = 20/(−5) = −4.

10. The line through (1, 12) and (5, k) has slope −3. What is k?

Answer: B) 0
−3 = (k − 12)/(5 − 1) = (k − 12)/4. Thus k − 12 = −12, so k = 0.

11. For f(x) = −1.25x + 6, what is the rate of change?

Answer: B) −1.25
For a linear function in slope-intercept form, the rate of change is the coefficient of x: −1.25.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. A temperature is 78°F at 2 p.m. and 60°F at 8 p.m. If the change is linear, what is the rate of change?

Answer: C) −3°F per hour
Rate = (60 − 78)/(8 − 2) = −18/6 = −3°F per hour.

13. A tank contains 92 liters after 1 minute and 52 liters after 5 minutes. If the amount changes linearly, what is the slope?

Answer: B) −10 liters/minute
Slope = (52 − 92)/(5 − 1) = −40/4 = −10 liters per minute.

14. Which equation represents the line through (0, 5) and (4, −3)?

Answer: A) y = −2x + 5
m = (−3 − 5)/(4 − 0) = −8/4 = −2. Since x = 0 gives y = 5, the intercept is 5, so y = −2x + 5.

15. A linear table contains (−2, 7), (1, 1), and (4, −5). Which statement correctly describes the slope?

Answer: A) The output decreases by 2 per input unit.
m = (1 − 7)/(1 − (−2)) = −6/3 = −2. The next pair also gives −6/3 = −2.

16. The line through (a, 14) and (10, 2) has slope −3/2. What is a?

Answer: A) 2
−3/2 = (2 − 14)/(10 − a) = −12/(10 − a). Cross-multiplying gives −3(10 − a) = −24, so 10 − a = 8 and a = 2.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. A line passes through (−5, 16) and (1, 4). If the same line also passes through (8, y), what is y?

Answer: B) −10
m = (4 − 16)/(1 − (−5)) = −12/6 = −2. From x = 1 to x = 8, Δx = 7, so Δy = −2(7) = −14. Therefore y = 4 − 14 = −10.

18. The line through (2, k) and (10, −7) has slope −5/4. What is k?

Answer: C) 3
−5/4 = (−7 − k)/(10 − 2) = (−7 − k)/8. Multiplying by 8 gives −10 = −7 − k, so k = 3.
