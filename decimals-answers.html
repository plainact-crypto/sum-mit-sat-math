13

A taxi charges a $3.25 starting fee plus $1.75 per mile. If the total fare is $15.50, how many miles were traveled?

Answer: 7 miles
3.25 + 1.75m = 15.501.75m = 12.25m = 7
14

A service bill is modeled by 0.8p + 6.4 = 30.4, where p is the pre-fee amount in dollars. What is p?

Answer: $30
0.8p = 24p = 30
15

A machine fills 0.45 of a batch in 18 minutes at a constant rate. How many minutes would it take to fill one whole batch?

Answer: B) 40 minutes
0.45T = 18T = 18 ÷ 0.45T = 40
16

A mobile plan charges $4.50 plus $0.12 per minute of extra use. If the extra-use bill is $10.50, how many extra minutes were used?

Answer: 50 minutes
4.50 + 0.12m = 10.500.12m = 6m = 50
