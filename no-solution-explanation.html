01 · LESSON OBJECTIVE

Recognize when a linear system has no solution.

Learn to identify inconsistent systems from equations, slopes, and graphs, then explain why no ordered pair can satisfy both equations.

same slope + different intercepts→parallel lines → no solution
02 · WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

A system needs a point that satisfies both equations.

If two distinct lines are parallel, they never meet. Because there is no shared point, there is no ordered pair that makes both equations true.

Graphno intersectionNo common point exists.
Slope cluesame slopeParallel lines have equal slopes.
Intercept cluedifferent interceptsThe equations represent different lines.
Algebra cluefalse statementElimination may end with something like 0 = 4.
03 · KEY DEFINITIONS

System

Two or more equations considered together.

Inconsistent System

A system with no common solution.

Parallel Lines

Distinct lines in a plane that have the same slope and never intersect.

04 · CORE RULE

Same slope and different y-intercepts means no solution.

m₁ = m₂ and b₁ ≠ b₂ → NO SOLUTION
05 · HOW IT WORKS

Check the relationship before doing extra algebra.

01
Put equations in comparable form.Slope-intercept form often makes the relationship easiest to see.
02
Compare slopes.If the slopes differ, the system has one solution instead.
03
Compare intercepts.Equal slopes with different intercepts identify distinct parallel lines.
04
Confirm algebraically.Elimination or substitution produces a contradiction.

11 · VISUAL MEANING

Parallel lines keep the same vertical separation.

For y = 2x + 1 and y = 2x − 3, the second line is always 4 units below the first. At x = 0 the y-values are 1 and −3; at x = 1 they are 3 and −1; at x = 2 they are 5 and 1. Because the difference never becomes zero, the lines never intersect.

12 · COMMON MISTAKES
Stopping at equal slopes

Equal slopes alone are not enough. Equal intercepts would mean the same line and infinitely many solutions.

Treating a contradiction as an algebra error

A correct result such as 0 = 4 is evidence that the system is inconsistent.

Comparing coefficients before matching forms

Rearrange or scale equations carefully before deciding their relationship.

13 · EXAM STRATEGY

Compare slopes before solving when the equations make that easy.

If both equations are already in slope-intercept form, recognizing equal slopes and different intercepts is faster than substitution or elimination. Use algebra when the relationship is not obvious.

15 · LESSON RECAP

No intersection means no solution.

  1. A solution must satisfy both equations.
  2. Distinct parallel lines have equal slopes and different intercepts.
  3. Parallel lines have no common point.
  4. Algebra confirms this with a false statement.
  5. Always distinguish no solution from infinitely many solutions.
