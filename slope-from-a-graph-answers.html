Slope from a Graph

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. On a coordinate graph, a line passes through (1, 2) and (3, 8). What is the slope?

Answer: B) 3
m = (8 − 2)/(3 − 1) = 6/2 = 3.

2. A graphed line passes through (−2, 5) and (2, −3). What is its slope?

Answer: B) −2
m = (−3 − 5)/(2 − (−2)) = −8/4 = −2.

3. A line on a graph passes through (0, −1) and (5, 4). What is the slope?

Answer: B) 1
m = (4 − (−1))/(5 − 0) = 5/5 = 1.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. A graphed line passes through (−4, −2) and (2, 7). What is the slope?

Answer: B) 3/2
m = (7 − (−2))/(2 − (−4)) = 9/6 = 3/2.

5. A line on a graph passes through (1, 6) and (5, 4). What is the slope?

Answer: B) −1/2
m = (4 − 6)/(5 − 1) = −2/4 = −1/2.

6. A graphed line passes through (−3, −4) and (−1, 2). What is the slope?

Answer: C) 3
m = (2 − (−4))/(−1 − (−3)) = 6/2 = 3.

7. A vertical line on a coordinate graph passes through (2, 5) and (2, −1). What is its slope?

Answer: D) undefined
The x-values are equal, so Δx = 0. Division by zero is undefined, so the slope is undefined.

8. A horizontal line passes through (−5, 3) and (4, 3). What is its slope?

Answer: B) 0
The y-values are equal, so Δy = 0. Thus m = 0/(4 − (−5)) = 0.

9. A graphed line passes through (0.5, 1) and (2.5, 6). What is the slope?

Answer: B) 2.5
m = (6 − 1)/(2.5 − 0.5) = 5/2 = 2.5.

10. A line passes through (−6, 8) and (−2, 2). What is the slope?

Answer: A) −3/2
m = (2 − 8)/(−2 − (−6)) = −6/4 = −3/2.

11. A graphed line passes through (−1, −2) and (3, 10). What is the slope?

Answer: B) 3
m = (10 − (−2))/(3 − (−1)) = 12/4 = 3.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. A line drawn on a grid rises 9 units while moving 12 units to the right. What is its slope?

Answer: A) 3/4
Slope = rise/run = 9/12 = 3/4.

13. A distance-time graph shows 15 km at 2 hours and 36 km at 5 hours. What is the average rate of change over this interval?

Answer: B) 7 km/h
Average rate of change = (36 − 15)/(5 − 2) = 21/3 = 7 km/h.

14. A temperature graph shows 18° at 4 minutes and 9° at 10 minutes. What is the rate of change?

Answer: A) −3/2° per minute
Rate = (9 − 18)/(10 − 4) = −9/6 = −3/2 degree per minute.

15. A graphed line passes through (−2, 1) and (4, 13). Which equation has the same slope and passes through these points?

Answer: B) y = 2x + 5
First find the slope: m = (13 − 1)/(4 − (−2)) = 12/6 = 2. Using (−2, 1), 1 = 2(−2) + b, so b = 5. Therefore y = 2x + 5.

16. A line on a graph passes through (1, 4) and (k, 16). If its slope is 4, what is k?

Answer: B) 4
Set 4 = (16 − 4)/(k − 1) = 12/(k − 1). Then 4(k − 1) = 12, so k − 1 = 3 and k = 4.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. A line passes through (−3, 7) and (5, −9). If the same line also passes through (9, y), what is y?

Answer: B) −17
The slope is (−9 − 7)/(5 − (−3)) = −16/8 = −2. From x = 5 to x = 9, Δx = 4, so Δy = −2(4) = −8. Thus y = −9 − 8 = −17.

18. A graphed line passes through (a, 2) and (7, 17). If its slope is 3, what is a?

Answer: B) 2
Set 3 = (17 − 2)/(7 − a) = 15/(7 − a). Then 3(7 − a) = 15, so 7 − a = 5 and a = 2.
