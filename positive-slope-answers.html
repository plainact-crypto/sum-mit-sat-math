Positive Slope

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. A line passes through (1, 2) and (4, 11). What is its slope?

Answer: B) 3
m = (11 − 2)/(4 − 1) = 9/3 = 3.

2. What is the slope of y = 0.75x − 4?

Answer: C) 0.75
In y = mx + b, the coefficient of x is the slope, so m = 0.75.

3. A table contains the points (−2, 1), (0, 5), and (2, 9). What is the slope?

Answer: B) 2
Using (−2,1) and (0,5), m = (5 − 1)/(0 − (−2)) = 4/2 = 2. The next pair gives the same rate.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. Find the slope through (−3, −5) and (5, 7).

Answer: B) 3/2
m = (7 − (−5))/(5 − (−3)) = 12/8 = 3/2.

5. Find the slope through (6, 14) and (2, 4).

Answer: B) 5/2
m = (4 − 14)/(2 − 6) = −10/−4 = 5/2.

6. What is the slope of y = (7/4)x + 2?

Answer: C) 7/4
The coefficient of x is the slope, so m = 7/4.

7. A line rises 10 units while moving 25 units to the right. What is its slope?

Answer: A) 2/5
Slope = rise/run = 10/25 = 2/5.

8. A table includes the points (1, −2) and (4, 7). What is the slope?

Answer: B) 3
m = (7 − (−2))/(4 − 1) = 9/3 = 3.

9. For a line, Δx = −6 and Δy = −15. What is the slope?

Answer: D) 5/2
m = Δy/Δx = (−15)/(−6) = 15/6 = 5/2.

10. The line through (2, 3) and (6, k) has slope 4. What is k?

Answer: C) 19
4 = (k − 3)/(6 − 2) = (k − 3)/4, so k − 3 = 16 and k = 19.

11. For f(x) = 1.2x + 9, what is the rate of change?

Answer: B) 1.2
For a linear function in slope-intercept form, the rate of change is the coefficient of x: 1.2.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. A savings balance is $120 after 2 weeks and $300 after 8 weeks. If the relationship is linear, what is the rate of increase?

Answer: B) $30 per week
Rate = (300 − 120)/(8 − 2) = 180/6 = 30 dollars per week.

13. A plant is 14 cm tall on day 3 and 32 cm tall on day 9. Assuming linear growth, what is the slope?

Answer: B) 3 cm/day
Slope = (32 − 14)/(9 − 3) = 18/6 = 3 cm per day.

14. Which equation represents the line through (0, −2) and (3, 4)?

Answer: B) y = 2x − 2
m = (4 − (−2))/(3 − 0) = 6/3 = 2. Since x = 0 gives y = −2, the intercept is −2, so y = 2x − 2.

15. A linear table contains (−1, 4), (2, 10), and (5, 16). Which statement correctly describes the slope?

Answer: B) The output increases by 2 per input unit.
m = (10 − 4)/(2 − (−1)) = 6/3 = 2. The next pair also gives 6/3 = 2.

16. The line through (a, 5) and (9, 17) has slope 3/2. What is a?

Answer: A) 1
3/2 = (17 − 5)/(9 − a) = 12/(9 − a). Cross-multiplying gives 3(9 − a) = 24, so 9 − a = 8 and a = 1.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. A line passes through (−4, −1) and (2, 11). If the same line also passes through (7, y), what is y?

Answer: C) 21
m = (11 − (−1))/(2 − (−4)) = 12/6 = 2. From x = 2 to x = 7, Δx = 5, so Δy = 2(5) = 10. Therefore y = 11 + 10 = 21.

18. The line through (3, k) and (11, 26) has positive slope 9/4. What is k?

Answer: B) 8
9/4 = (26 − k)/(11 − 3) = (26 − k)/8. Thus 4(26 − k) = 72, so 26 − k = 18 and k = 8.
