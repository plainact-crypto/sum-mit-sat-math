Opens Up or Down

01 · LESSON OBJECTIVE

Use the leading coefficient to identify a parabola's opening direction.

For a quadratic in standard form, the sign of the coefficient of x² determines whether the graph opens upward or downward.

y=ax²+bx+c→a>0: up · a<0: down
02 · WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

The x² coefficient controls direction.

A quadratic function has degree 2 and its graph is a parabola. In y=ax²+bx+c, a is the leading coefficient and a≠0.

03 · KEY DEFINITIONS

Leading coefficient

The coefficient a multiplying x².

Opens upward

The arms rise and the vertex is a minimum.

Opens downward

The arms fall and the vertex is a maximum.

04 · CORE RULE

Check only the sign of a.

a > 0 → opens up · a < 0 → opens down
05 · HOW IT WORKS
01
Write in standard form.Identify the x² term.
02
Read a.Include its sign.
03
Classify.Positive means up; negative means down.

10 · GRAPH / VISUAL BLOCK

The sign of a flips the parabola.

Checks: y=x² gives (−1,1),(0,0),(1,1); y=−x² gives (−1,−1),(0,0),(1,−1).

12 · COMMON MISTAKES
Looking at b

The x coefficient does not determine opening direction.

Looking at c

c gives the y-intercept, not direction.

Missing the implied 1

x² means 1x².

Forgetting to rearrange

Identify a only after the function is written with y or f(x) isolated.

13 · EXAM STRATEGY

Find a before doing any other quadratic work.

If a question asks only whether the parabola opens up or down, or whether its vertex is a maximum or minimum, do not calculate the vertex or roots. Read the sign of a.

15 · LESSON RECAP
  1. Write the quadratic in standard form.
  2. Identify a, the x² coefficient.
  3. a>0 means opens upward and has a minimum.
  4. a<0 means opens downward and has a maximum.
