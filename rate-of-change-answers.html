Rate of Change

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. Rate of change between (1, 4) and (5, 16)

Answer: B) 3
Rate of change = (16 − 4)/(5 − 1) = 12/4 = 3.

2. Rate of change between (−2, 7) and (2, −1)

Answer: B) −2
Rate of change = (−1 − 7)/(2 − (−2)) = −8/4 = −2.

3. Rate of change of y = 5x − 9

Answer: C) 5
In y = mx + b, the coefficient m is the rate of change. Here m = 5.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. Rate of change between (3, 10) and (9, 22)

Answer: B) 2
(22 − 10)/(9 − 3) = 12/6 = 2.

5. Rate of change between (2, 5) and (8, 8)

Answer: A) 1/2
(8 − 5)/(8 − 2) = 3/6 = 1/2.

6. Rate of change between (−3, 12) and (1, 4)

Answer: B) −2
(4 − 12)/(1 − (−3)) = −8/4 = −2.

7. Table with x = 0, 3, 6 and y = 7, 7, 7

Answer: B) 0
The y-value does not change, so Δy = 0. Therefore the rate of change is 0.

8. Rate of change of y = (−3/4)x + 6

Answer: C) −3/4
The coefficient of x is −3/4, so the rate of change is −3/4.

9. Rate of change between (1.5, 2) and (3.5, 7)

Answer: C) 2.5
(7 − 2)/(3.5 − 1.5) = 5/2 = 2.5.

10. Earnings from $45 after 2 hours to $105 after 5 hours

Answer: B) $20 per hour
Change in earnings is $60 over 3 hours, so 60/3 = $20 per hour.

11. Table with x = −2, 2, 6 and y = 9, 3, −3

Answer: A) −3/2
Using the first two points, (3 − 9)/(2 − (−2)) = −6/4 = −3/2. The next interval gives the same rate.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. Tank from 84 liters at 3 minutes to 60 liters at 9 minutes

Answer: B) −4 liters per minute
(60 − 84)/(9 − 3) = −24/6 = −4 liters per minute.

13. Cyclist from 35 km at 0.5 hour to 95 km at 2.5 hours

Answer: B) 30 km/h
Distance changes by 60 km while time changes by 2 hours, so 60/2 = 30 km/h.

14. Cost from $38 for 4 items to $74 for 10 items

Answer: B) $6 per item
Cost changes by $36 for 6 additional items, so 36/6 = $6 per item.

15. C = 120 − 8t

Answer: D) C decreases by 8 units each minute
The coefficient of t is −8. A negative rate means C decreases by 8 units for every 1-minute increase in t.

16. Rate of change 5/2 through (2, 7); find y when x = 8

Answer: C) 22
From x = 2 to x = 8, Δx = 6. Then Δy = (5/2)(6) = 15, so y = 7 + 15 = 22.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. Rate of change −3/4 through (−2, 11) and (6, y)

Answer: B) 5
Δx = 6 − (−2) = 8. With rate −3/4, Δy = (−3/4)(8) = −6. Therefore y = 11 − 6 = 5.

18. Linear table through (−5, 14), (−1, 8), and (3, 2); find y when x = 9

Answer: B) −7
The rate is (8 − 14)/(−1 − (−5)) = −6/4 = −3/2. From x = 3 to x = 9, Δx = 6, so Δy = (−3/2)(6) = −9. Thus y = 2 − 9 = −7.
