Questions 12–16 from Practice Problems.

12

If 4x + 9 = 37, what is the value of x?

Answer: B) 7
4x = 28x = 7
13

A delivery service charges a $6 base fee plus $3 per package. If the total charge is $30, how many packages were delivered?

Answer: 8 packages
6 + 3p = 303p = 24p = 8
14

A rectangle has width x − 2 and length 5. If its area is 35 square units, what is x?

Answer: x = 9
5(x − 2) = 35x − 2 = 7x = 9
15

Which value of x satisfies 2x + 7 = x + 19?

Answer: C) 12
x + 7 = 19x = 12
16

A streaming plan costs $12 plus $0.80 per downloaded movie. If the bill is $28, how many movies were downloaded?

Answer: 20 movies
12 + 0.80m = 280.80m = 16m = 20
