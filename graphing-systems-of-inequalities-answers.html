B) y≥x and y≤x+2
At (1,2), 2≥1 and 2≤3.

10. Describe the overlap

B) above both lines
Both conditions require y to be on or above their boundaries.

11. Vertical and horizontal restrictions

C) (2, 0)

03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. Feasible region

C) (3, 5)

13. Find an intersection

B) (4, 3)
0.5x+1=−x+7 → 1.5x=6 → x=4, y=3.

14. Parallel boundaries with no overlap

A) y>2x+3 and y<2x+1
The first boundary is always 2 units above the second, so the strict regions cannot overlap.

15. School order constraints

C) (20, 20)
20+20=40≤40 and 40+20=60≤60.

16. Find the overlap point

C) (3, 3)
3>−2(3)+6=0 and 3≤3.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. Region between parallel lines

18. Greatest allowed x-value

x+2≤−2x+8 → 3x≤6 → x≤2; with x≥0, the greatest x is 2.
