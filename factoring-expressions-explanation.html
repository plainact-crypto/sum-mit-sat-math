Factoring Expressions
Advanced Math
01 · LESSON OBJECTIVE

Rewrite a sum or difference as a product without changing its value.

Factoring reverses the distributive property. Instead of multiplying a factor through parentheses, you identify a factor shared by every term and place it outside the grouping symbols.

12x + 18→6(2x + 3)
02 · CORE IDEA

Find the greatest common factor, then divide every term by it.

ab + ac = a(b + c)

Best habit: after factoring, distribute your answer once mentally. If it does not reproduce the original expression exactly, the factorization is not correct.

03 · WORKED EXAMPLE 1 — NUMBER GCF

Factor completely: 15x + 25

GCF(15,25) = 515x ÷ 5 = 3x, 25 ÷ 5 = 515x + 25 = 5(3x + 5)
Math cross-checkDistribute: 5(3x+5)=15x+25.
04 · WORKED EXAMPLE 2 — VARIABLE GCF

Factor completely: 18x² + 12x

number GCF = 6variable GCF = x18x² + 12x = 6x(3x + 2)

For variables, factor out the lowest power present in every term. Both terms contain at least one factor of x.

Math cross-check6x(3x+2)=18x²+12x.
05 · WORKED EXAMPLE 3 — THREE TERMS

Factor completely: 14a²b + 21ab² - 7ab

common number factor = 7common variable factor = ab7ab(2a + 3b - 1)
Math cross-checkDistributing 7ab gives 14a²b + 21ab² - 7ab term by term.
06 · NEGATIVE LEADING TERM

Factor: -8y² + 20y

-4y(2y - 5)

You may factor out a negative common factor when it makes the first term inside parentheses positive. This is often easier to read and reduces sign mistakes.

Math cross-check-4y(2y-5)=-8y²+20y.
07 · A RELIABLE METHOD
  1. Find the greatest common numerical factor of all coefficients.
  2. For each variable shared by every term, use the smallest exponent.
  3. Write the complete GCF outside parentheses.
  4. Divide every original term by the GCF to build the expression inside.
  5. Check by distributing.
08 · COMMON MISTAKES
Using a factor that is not common to every term

A factor can leave the parentheses only if it divides every term.

Forgetting a term after division

Factoring does not remove terms. Every original term must have a matching quotient inside the parentheses.

Stopping before the GCF is greatest

12x+18=2(6x+9) is equivalent, but 6(2x+3) is factored completely by the GCF.

Losing a negative sign

If you factor out a negative value, every sign inside the parentheses changes accordingly.

09 · EXAM STRATEGY

Use divisibility first, then scan variable powers.

For an exam-style GCF question, start with the coefficients, then inspect each variable. This two-pass method is faster and safer than guessing the full factor at once.

11 · LESSON RECAP
  1. Factoring is the reverse of distribution.
  2. The GCF must divide every term.
  3. For shared variables, factor out the smallest exponent.
  4. Factoring out a negative can make the inside expression easier to read.
  5. Distribution is the fastest exact check of a factorization.
