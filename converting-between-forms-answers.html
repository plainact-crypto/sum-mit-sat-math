ANSWERS & SOLUTIONS

Converting Between Forms

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. Rewrite x + y = 9 in slope-intercept form.

Answer: B) y = −x + 9
Subtract x from both sides: y = 9 − x = −x + 9.

2. Rewrite y = 3x − 5 in standard form.

Answer: A) 3x − y = 5
Move y to the left and 5 to the right: 3x − y = 5.

3. Which equation is equivalent to y − 4 = 2(x − 3)?

Answer: A) y = 2x − 2
Distribute: y − 4 = 2x − 6. Add 4: y = 2x − 2.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. Rewrite 2x + y = 11 in slope-intercept form.

Answer: B) y = −2x + 11
Subtract 2x: y = −2x + 11.

5. Rewrite 4x + 2y = 12 in slope-intercept form.

Answer: A) y = −2x + 6
2y = −4x + 12, then divide by 2.

6. Rewrite y = −5x + 8 in standard form with a positive x coefficient.

Answer: A) 5x + y = 8
Add 5x to both sides.

7. Rewrite y = (1/2)x − 3 in standard form using integer coefficients.

Answer: A) x − 2y = 6
Multiply by 2: 2y = x − 6. Rearrange: x − 2y = 6.

8. Rewrite y + 1 = 3(x − 2) in slope-intercept form.

Answer: A) y = 3x − 7
Distribute: y + 1 = 3x − 6. Subtract 1.

9. Rewrite y − 6 = −2(x + 1) in slope-intercept form.

Answer: A) y = −2x + 4
Distribute: y − 6 = −2x − 2. Add 6.

10. The line y = 4x + 1 passes through (2, 9). Which point-slope form uses that point?

Answer: A) y − 9 = 4(x − 2)
Use y − y₁ = m(x − x₁) with m = 4 and (x₁,y₁) = (2,9).

11. Rewrite 3x − y = 7 in slope-intercept form.

Answer: A) y = 3x − 7
−y = 7 − 3x, then multiply by −1.
03 · SAT-STYLE PRACTICE

12. A line is given by 6x + 2y = 18. What is its slope?

Answer: B) −3
2y = −6x + 18, so y = −3x + 9. The slope is −3.

13. A line is given by 5x − y = 12. What is its y-intercept?

Answer: A) −12
−y = 12 − 5x, so y = 5x − 12. The y-intercept is −12.

14. A service cost is modeled by y = 7x + 25. Which standard-form equation is equivalent?

Answer: A) 7x − y = −25
Subtract y and 25 appropriately: 7x − y = −25.

15. A line has slope −3 and passes through (4, 10). Which equation is an equivalent point-slope form?

Answer: A) y − 10 = −3(x − 4)
Substitute m = −3 and (4,10) into y − y₁ = m(x − x₁).

16. The equation 2x + 4y = 20 models a linear relationship. What is the y-intercept?

Answer: C) 5
Set x = 0: 4y = 20, so y = 5.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. Which equation is equivalent to 3(y − 2) = −6(x + 1)?

Answer: A) y = −2x
3y − 6 = −6x − 6. Add 6: 3y = −6x. Divide by 3.

18. A line passes through (−2, 5) and has slope 3/2. Which standard-form equation represents the line?

Answer: A) 3x − 2y = −16
Start with y − 5 = (3/2)(x + 2). Multiply by 2: 2y − 10 = 3x + 6. Rearrange: 3x − 2y = −16.
