Slope from a Table

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. A table contains the points (1, 5) and (3, 11). What is the slope?

Answer: B) 3
Use m = Δy/Δx. Here Δy = 11 − 5 = 6 and Δx = 3 − 1 = 2, so m = 6/2 = 3.

2. A table contains the points (−2, 9) and (2, 1). What is the slope?

Answer: B) −2
m = (1 − 9)/(2 − (−2)) = −8/4 = −2.

3. A table contains the points (0, 4) and (5, 14). What is the slope?

Answer: B) 2
m = (14 − 4)/(5 − 0) = 10/5 = 2.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. For x-values 2 and 6, the corresponding y-values are 7 and 15. What is the slope?

Answer: B) 2
m = (15 − 7)/(6 − 2) = 8/4 = 2.

5. For x-values −3 and 0, the corresponding y-values are 10 and 4. What is the slope?

Answer: B) −2
m = (4 − 10)/(0 − (−3)) = −6/3 = −2.

6. For x-values 1 and 5, the corresponding y-values are 9 and 7. What is the slope?

Answer: B) −1/2
m = (7 − 9)/(5 − 1) = −2/4 = −1/2.

7. A table shows x = 0, 4, 8 and y = −3, −3, −3. What is the slope?

Answer: B) 0
The y-value does not change. Therefore Δy = 0 for every nonzero Δx, so the slope is 0.

8. A table contains (−4, 1) and (2, 13). What is the slope?

Answer: B) 2
m = (13 − 1)/(2 − (−4)) = 12/6 = 2.

9. A table contains (0.5, 2) and (1.5, 5). What is the slope?

Answer: C) 3
m = (5 − 2)/(1.5 − 0.5) = 3/1 = 3.

10. A table contains (2, −1) and (8, −4). What is the slope?

Answer: B) −1/2
m = (−4 − (−1))/(8 − 2) = −3/6 = −1/2.

11. A linear table shows x = −1, 3, 7 and y = 6, 0, −6. What is the slope?

Answer: A) −3/2
Using the first two rows, m = (0 − 6)/(3 − (−1)) = −6/4 = −3/2. The next pair gives the same ratio, confirming the constant slope.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. A delivery vehicle has traveled 12 km after 1 hour and 30 km after 4 hours. What is the average rate of change in distance over this interval?

Answer: B) 6 km/h
Average rate of change = (30 − 12)/(4 − 1) = 18/3 = 6 km/h.

13. A temperature table shows 72° at 0 minutes and 67° at 10 minutes. What is the rate of change of temperature?

Answer: B) −1/2° per minute
Rate = (67 − 72)/(10 − 0) = −5/10 = −1/2 degree per minute.

14. A linear table shows x = 2, 6, 10 and y = 5, 8, 11. What is the slope?

Answer: B) 3/4
From x = 2 to x = 6, Δx = 4 while Δy = 8 − 5 = 3, so m = 3/4. The next pair has the same changes.

15. A table shows x = 0, 2, 4, 6 and y = 1, 5, 9, 13. Which statement is true?

Answer: B) The slope is 2
Each step increases x by 2 and y by 4. Thus m = 4/2 = 2, and the slope is constant.

16. A linear relationship has the points (2, 11) and (5, y). If the slope is −3, what is y?

Answer: B) 2
Use −3 = (y − 11)/(5 − 2) = (y − 11)/3. Then y − 11 = −9, so y = 2.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. A table contains the points (1, 4) and (k, 16). If the slope is 3, what is k?

Answer: B) 5
Set 3 = (16 − 4)/(k − 1) = 12/(k − 1). Then 3(k − 1) = 12, so k − 1 = 4 and k = 5.

18. A linear table contains (−2, 12), (1, 6), and (5, −2). What is the y-value when x = 8?

Answer: B) −8
The slope from (−2, 12) to (1, 6) is (6 − 12)/(1 − (−2)) = −6/3 = −2. From x = 5 to x = 8, Δx = 3, so Δy = −2(3) = −6. Starting from y = −2 gives y = −2 − 6 = −8.
