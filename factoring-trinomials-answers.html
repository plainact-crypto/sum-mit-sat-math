ANSWERS & SOLUTIONS

Factoring Trinomials

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

1. Factor completely: x² + 7x + 12

Find two integers whose product is 12 and sum is 7: 3 and 4. Expanding (x+3)(x+4) gives x²+7x+12.
Answer: A) (x + 3)(x + 4)

2. Factor completely: x² - 9x + 20

The factors of 20 that add to -9 are -4 and -5. Their product expands back to the original trinomial.
Answer: A) (x - 4)(x - 5)

3. Factor completely: x² + 2x - 15

5 and -3 multiply to -15 and add to 2.
Answer: A) (x + 5)(x - 3)

4. Factor: x² + 11x + 24

3·8=24 and 3+8=11.
Answer: A) (x + 3)(x + 8)

5. Factor: x² - 5x - 24

-8·3=-24 and -8+3=-5.
Answer: A) (x - 8)(x + 3)

6. Factor: x² - 13x + 40

-5 and -8 multiply to 40 and add to -13.
Answer: A) (x - 5)(x - 8)

7. Factor: 2x² + 7x + 3

Expand (2x+1)(x+3): 2x²+6x+x+3=2x²+7x+3.
Answer: A) (2x + 1)(x + 3)

8. Factor: 3x² - 10x - 8

Expand (3x+2)(x-4): 3x²-12x+2x-8=3x²-10x-8.
Answer: A) (3x + 2)(x - 4)

9. Factor: 4x² + 12x + 5

Expand (2x+1)(2x+5): 4x²+10x+2x+5=4x²+12x+5.
Answer: A) (2x + 1)(2x + 5)

10. Factor completely: 6x² + 15x + 9

First factor the GCF 3: 3(2x²+5x+3). Then 2x²+5x+3=(2x+3)(x+1).
Answer: B) 3(2x + 3)(x + 1)

11. Factor: 5x² - 13x + 6

Expand (5x-3)(x-2): 5x²-10x-3x+6=5x²-13x+6.
Answer: A) (5x - 3)(x - 2)

12. Which is the complete factorization of 2x² - x - 6?

(2x+3)(x-2)=2x²-4x+3x-6=2x²-x-6.
Answer: A) (2x + 3)(x - 2)

13. A rectangle has area x² + 9x + 20 square units. What are its side lengths?

Factor the area: x²+9x+20=(x+4)(x+5), since 4·5=20 and 4+5=9.
Answer: A) x + 4 and x + 5

14. For x² + kx + 18 = (x + 3)(x + 6), find k.

Expand the right side: x²+9x+18. Matching the x-coefficient gives k=9.
Answer: A) 9

15. Factor completely: 12x² - 11x - 5

(3x+1)(4x-5)=12x²-15x+4x-5=12x²-11x-5.
Answer: A) (3x + 1)(4x - 5)

16. The trinomial x² + bx - 28 factors as (x + 7)(x - 4). What is b?

Expand: (x+7)(x-4)=x²+3x-28. Therefore b=3.
Answer: A) 3

17. Factor completely: 6x² - x - 12

(3x+4)(2x-3)=6x²-9x+8x-12=6x²-x-12.
Answer: A) (3x + 4)(2x - 3)

18. For positive integer n, factor x^(2n) - 5x^n - 24.

Let u=x^n. Then u²-5u-24=(u-8)(u+3). Substitute u=x^n back into the factors.
Answer: A) (x^n - 8)(x^n + 3)
