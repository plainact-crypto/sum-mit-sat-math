Questions 12–16 from Practice Problems.

12

What value of x satisfies x/3 + 5/6 = 17/6?

Answer: B) 6
2x + 5 = 172x = 12x = 6
13

A container is 2/5 full. After 12 liters are added, it is 3/5 full. What is the total capacity?

Answer: 60 liters
(3/5 − 2/5)C = 12(1/5)C = 12C = 60
14

A tank is 1/4 full. After 18 gallons are added, it is 5/8 full. What is the total capacity?

Answer: 48 gallons
(5/8 − 1/4)T = 18(3/8)T = 18T = 48
15

Which value of x satisfies (x + 4)/6 = (x − 2)/3?

Answer: C) 8
x + 4 = 2(x − 2)x + 4 = 2x − 4x = 8
16

A fundraiser has collected 1/3 of its goal. After a $40 donation, it has collected 1/2 of its goal. What is the goal?

Answer: $240
(1/2 − 1/3)G = 40(1/6)G = 40G = 240
