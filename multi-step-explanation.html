Solving Multi-Step Linear Equations
Algebra
01 · LESSON OBJECTIVE

Simplify first. Then isolate the variable.

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to solve linear equations that take more than one inverse operation by simplifying the expression, undoing addition or subtraction, and then undoing multiplication or division.

3x + 7 = 25→x = 6
02 · WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

A multi-step equation is still a balance.

The goal has not changed: get the variable alone. The difference is that more than one operation is attached to the variable, so you remove those operations in a logical order while keeping both sides equal.

FirstSimplifyDistribute and combine like terms when needed.
NextUndo + / −Move the constant away from the variable term.
LastUndo × / ÷Make the coefficient of the variable equal to 1.
AlwaysCheckSubstitute your answer into the original equation.
03 · KEY DEFINITIONS

Coefficient

The number multiplying a variable.

5x → coefficient 5

Constant

A number with no variable attached.

5x + 9 → constant 9

Like Terms

Terms with the same variable part that can be combined.

4x + 3x = 7x

Distributive Property

Multiply a factor by every term inside parentheses.

3(x + 2) = 3x + 6
04 · CORE RULE

Simplify the equation before you start undoing operations.

SIMPLIFY → ISOLATE → CHECK
05 · HOW IT WORKS

A reliable four-step process

01
Simplify each side.Distribute and combine like terms if necessary.
02
Undo addition or subtraction.Move the constant away from the variable term.
03
Undo multiplication or division.Divide or multiply so the variable has coefficient 1.
04
Check the solution.Substitute it into the original equation.
06 · WORKED EXAMPLE 1 — BASIC

Solve: 2x + 7 = 19

  1. Undo the addition.Subtract 7 from both sides.2x + 7 − 7 = 19 − 7
  2. Simplify.2x = 12
  3. Undo the multiplication.Divide both sides by 2.x = 6
Check2(6) + 7 = 19 ✓
07 · WORKED EXAMPLE 2 — NEGATIVE CONSTANT

Solve: 5x − 8 = 27

5x − 8 + 8 = 27 + 85x = 35x = 7
Check5(7) − 8 = 27 ✓
08 · WORKED EXAMPLE 3 — DISTRIBUTIVE PROPERTY

Solve: 3(x − 4) + 5 = 20

3x − 12 + 5 = 203x − 7 = 203x = 27x = 9

Key move: Distribute first, then combine the constants before isolating x.

09 · WORKED EXAMPLE 4 — EXAM STYLE

Solve: 4(2x − 3) + 5 = 25

8x − 12 + 5 = 258x − 7 = 258x = 32x = 4
Exam connectionDo not rush past the parentheses. A missed distribution sign creates a wrong equation before the real solving even begins.
10 · WORKED EXAMPLE 5 — HARDER SIGN CONTROL

Solve: 6 − 2(x + 3) = −10

6 − 2x − 6 = −10−2x = −10x = 5
Check6 − 2(5 + 3) = −10 ✓

11 · COMMON MISTAKES
Skipping simplification

If parentheses or like terms are present, simplify them before isolating the variable.

Distributing to only one term

In 3(x − 4), multiply both x and −4 by 3.

Losing a negative sign

In −2(x + 3), the result is −2x − 6, not −2x + 6.

Dividing too early

For 5x − 8 = 27, remove −8 before dividing by 5.

12 · EXAM STRATEGY

Do the cleanup before the algebra.

When an exam-style equation contains parentheses or several terms, spend a few seconds simplifying first. A clean equation is easier to solve and easier to check.

2(3x + 1) − 4 = 22→6x − 2 = 22 → x = 4

14 · LESSON RECAP

Multi-step does not mean complicated.

  1. Simplify parentheses and like terms first.
  2. Undo addition or subtraction.
  3. Undo multiplication or division.
  4. Keep both sides balanced.
  5. Substitute your answer to check.
