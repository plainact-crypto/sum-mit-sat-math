Greatest Common Factor

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

1. Factor completely: 12x + 18

GCF(12,18)=6. Divide both terms by 6: 12x/6=2x and 18/6=3.
Answer: A) 6(2x + 3)

2. Factor completely: 15a² + 25a

The numerical GCF is 5 and both terms contain a. Factoring 5a leaves 3a+5.
Answer: A) 5a(3a + 5)

3. Factor completely: 8m³ - 20m²

GCF(8,20)=4 and the smallest power is m². Factoring 4m² leaves 2m-5.
Answer: A) 4m²(2m - 5)

4. Factor: 18x³y² + 24x²y³

GCF=6x²y². The quotients are 3x and 4y.
Answer: A) 6x²y²(3x + 4y)

5. Factor: 20a³b - 30a²b² + 10ab

All terms share 10ab. Dividing term-by-term gives 2a²-3ab+1.
Answer: A) 10ab(2a² - 3ab + 1)

6. Factor: 14m²n + 21mn² + 35m

The coefficient GCF is 7 and m appears in every term. Factoring 7m gives 2mn+3n²+5.
Answer: A) 7m(2mn + 3n² + 5)

7. Factor: 24p⁴q² - 36p³q⁵

GCF=12p³q², using the smaller exponent of each variable. The remaining terms are 2p and -3q³.
Answer: A) 12p³q²(2p - 3q³)

8. Factor: 27r³s + 45r²s²

GCF=9r²s. Dividing leaves 3r+5s.
Answer: A) 9r²s(3r + 5s)

9. Factor: 32x⁵ - 48x³ + 16x²

GCF=16x². The quotients are 2x³, -3x, and 1.
Answer: A) 16x²(2x³ - 3x + 1)

10. Factor: 21u²v³ - 14uv² + 35uv

GCF=7uv. Dividing gives 3uv²-2v+5.
Answer: A) 7uv(3uv² - 2v + 5)

11. Factor: -15a²b + 25ab²

Both terms share 5ab. Factoring it gives -3a+5b.
Answer: A) 5ab(-3a + 5b)

12. Complete factorization of 42x³ - 56x²?

GCF(42,56)=14 and both terms contain x². The remaining binomial is 3x-4.
Answer: A) 14x²(3x - 4)

13. A rectangle has area 18x² + 30x and width 6x. Its length?

Length=area/width=(18x²+30x)/(6x)=3x+5. Multiplying 6x(3x+5) returns the stated area.
Answer: A) 3x + 5

14. GCF of 36a⁴b³ and 54a²b⁵?

gcd(36,54)=18. Use the smaller exponents: a² and b³.
Answer: A) 18a²b³

15. Factor: 16t³ - 40t² + 24t

GCF=8t. Dividing each term gives 2t²-5t+3.
Answer: A) 8t(2t² - 5t + 3)

16. 12x²y + 18xy² = kxy(2x + 3y). Find k.

Factor the left side: 6xy(2x+3y). Matching factors gives k=6.
Answer: A) 6

17. For positive integer n, factor: 18x^(n+2) + 30x^(n+1) - 12x^n

The coefficient GCF is 6 and the smallest x power is x^n. Factoring 6x^n leaves 3x²+5x-2.
Answer: A) 6x^n(3x² + 5x - 2)

18. If the GCF of 24x^a y³ and 36x⁵y^b is 12x³y², what must hold?

The variable exponent in a GCF is the smaller exponent. Therefore min(a,5)=3 and min(3,b)=2. These conditions force a=3 and b=2, and the coefficient GCF is gcd(24,36)=12.
Answer: A) min(a,5)=3 and min(3,b)=2
