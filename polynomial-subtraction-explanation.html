Polynomial Subtraction
Advanced Math
01 · LESSON OBJECTIVE

Subtract polynomials accurately by distributing the subtraction sign first.

Subtracting a polynomial means subtracting every term inside the second polynomial. The safest method is to change the sign of each term in the second polynomial, then combine like terms.

(5x² + 3x - 4) - (2x² - 7x + 6)→3x² + 10x - 10
02 · CORE RULE

Distribute −1 across the entire polynomial being subtracted.

A - (B + C - D) = A - B - C + D

Key habit: rewrite the subtraction before combining anything. A sign change applies to every term in the second parentheses.

03 · A RELIABLE THREE-STEP METHOD

1 · Keep the first polynomial

5x² + 3x - 4

Do not change its signs.

2 · Opposite every sign

-(2x² - 7x + 6) = -2x² + 7x - 6

The outside minus multiplies every coefficient by −1.

3 · Combine like terms

3x² + 10x - 10

Only terms with identical variables and exponents combine.

04 · WORKED EXAMPLE 1

Subtract (2x² - 7x + 6) from (5x² + 3x - 4).

(5x² + 3x - 4) - (2x² - 7x + 6)5x² + 3x - 4 - 2x² + 7x - 63x² + 10x - 10
Math cross-checkAt x=2, the first polynomial is 22 and the second is 0, so their difference is 22. The result gives 3(4)+10(2)-10=22.
05 · WORKED EXAMPLE 2 — MISSING POWERS

Find (4a³ - 2a + 5) - (a³ + 3a² - 6a - 1).

4a³ - 2a + 5 - a³ - 3a² + 6a + 13a³ - 3a² + 4a + 6

The missing a² term in the first polynomial has coefficient zero. It does not disappear from the subtraction process; it simply contributes nothing.

Math cross-checkAt a=1, the original polynomials are 7 and -3, so 7-(-3)=10. The result gives 3-3+4+6=10.
06 · WORKED EXAMPLE 3 — APPLICATION

A larger region has area 6x² + 5x + 8 square units and a cutout has area 2x² + 9x + 3 square units. Find the remaining area.

(6x² + 5x + 8) - (2x² + 9x + 3)4x² - 4x + 5

“Remaining” means subtract the cutout area from the larger area, and the final unit remains square units.

Math cross-checkAt x=2, the areas are 42 and 29 square units, so the difference is 13. The result gives 4(4)-4(2)+5=13.
07 · VERTICAL ORGANIZATION

Line up equal powers when expressions are long.

6x³ + 2x² - 5x + 4-(2x³ - 3x² + x - 7)= 4x³ + 5x² - 6x + 11

Think of the second row as becoming -2x³ + 3x² - x + 7 before the columns are combined.

08 · COMMON MISTAKES
Changing only the first sign

In -(x²-4x+3), all three signs change: -x²+4x-3.

Subtracting exponents

7x²-2x²=5x², not 5x⁰. Like-term subtraction changes coefficients, not exponents.

Combining unlike powers

3x³-2x² cannot be reduced to one term.

Losing a negative constant

Write the sign change explicitly before doing mental arithmetic.

09 · EXAM STRATEGY

Rewrite first; simplify second.

On an exam-style problem, use one line only to distribute the negative sign. Then combine x³ terms, x² terms, x terms, and constants. Separating those two actions prevents most sign errors.

11 · LESSON RECAP
  1. Keep the first polynomial unchanged.
  2. Distribute −1 to every term of the polynomial being subtracted.
  3. Combine only like terms.
  4. Missing powers can be treated as zero-coefficient terms.
  5. Check signs before arranging the final polynomial in descending powers.
