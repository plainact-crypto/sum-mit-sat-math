Systems of Inequalities Word Problems

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. Theater constraints

Answer: A) x + y ≤ 120 and x ≥ 30
“At most 120 people” gives x+y≤120. “At least 30 adult tickets” gives x≥30.

2. Notebook and folder purchase

Answer: A) 8 notebooks and 6 folders
Cost = 3(8)+2(6)=36≤42, and the total number of items is 8+6=14≥10. Each other option breaks the budget or minimum-item condition.

3. Fundraiser constraints

Answer: A) 5x + 3y ≥ 90 and x + y ≤ 24
Revenue must be at least $90, so 5x+3y≥90. No more than 24 packs means x+y≤24.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. Field-trip vehicle plan

Answer: A) 2 vans and 6 buses
Seats = 8(2)+20(6)=136≥120, and vehicles = 2+6=8≤8.

5. Bakery production plan

Answer: A) 30 muffins and 20 cookies
Items = 50≤60 and profit = 2(30)+1.5(20)=90. The other options miss the profit target or exceed 60 items.

6. Study-time plan

Answer: A) x = 6, y = 2
6+2=8≤8 and 6≥2(2)=4, so both constraints hold.

7. School-event ticket system

Answer: A) x + y ≤ 60 and 12x + 7y ≥ 500
Capacity gives x+y≤60. The required minimum revenue gives 12x+7y≥500.

8. Art-kit combination

Answer: A) (10, 12)
10+12=22≤25, with 10≥8 red pieces and 12≥10 blue pieces.

9. Delivery-cart load

Answer: A) 14 small and 10 large
There are 24 boxes, and the weight is 4(14)+7(10)=126 kg≤140 kg.

10. Garden plan

Answer: A) 8 rose and 8 herb plots
Area = 3(8)+2(8)=40≤48 m², total plots =16≥12, and herb plots =8≥4.

11. Data-plan pair

Answer: A) 6 video and 5 music units
Usage points = 2(6)+5=17≤18 and total units = 6+5=11≥10.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. Concert ticket plan

Answer: A) 50 VIP and 150 regular
Attendance =200≤200, revenue =50(50)+30(150)=7000, and VIP tickets =50≥40.

13. Greatest integer parameter k

Answer: C) 3
For (20,10), the second constraint becomes 40+10k≤70, so 10k≤30 and k≤3. The first constraint also holds because 20+10=30≤35.

14. Required value of y

Answer: C) 12
With x=12, 4x+6y≤120 gives 48+6y≤120, so y≤12. Also 12+y≥24 gives y≥12. Therefore y=12.

15. Strict room-capacity plan

Answer: B) 25 adults and 20 students
25+20=45<50 and revenue =10(25)+5(20)=350≥350. Plans totaling 50 fail the strict capacity limit.

16. Test the workshop point

Answer: C) It satisfies both constraints
At (10,12), x+2y=10+24=34≤40 and 3x+y=30+12=42≤45.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. Count integer ordered pairs

Answer: D) 10
Because x≥4, y≥3, and x+y≤10, x can be 4,5,6,7. The allowed y-counts are respectively 4,3,2,1, totaling 4+3+2+1=10 ordered pairs.

18. Maximize 3x + 4y

Answer: C) 42
On x+y=12, write x=12−y. The second constraint gives 2(12−y)+3y=24+y≤30, so y≤6. The objective becomes 3(12−y)+4y=36+y, maximized at y=6, x=6, giving 42. Checking integer points beyond y=6 under 2x+3y≤30 gives no larger value.
