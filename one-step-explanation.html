Solving One-Step Linear Equations
Algebra
01 · LESSON OBJECTIVE

Get the variable by itself.

Solve one-step linear equations accurately by identifying the operation attached to the variable and undoing it.

x + 7 = 12→x = 5
02 · WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

An equation is a balance.

An equation says two expressions have equal value. Any operation applied to one side must also be applied to the other.

+ aUndo with − a
− aUndo with + a
axDivide by a
x/aMultiply by a
03 · KEY DEFINITIONS

Vocabulary

Variable: a letter representing an unknown value. Solution: a value that makes the equation true. Inverse operations: operations that undo each other, such as addition/subtraction and multiplication/division.

04 · CORE RULE

Whatever you do to one side, do the same thing to the other side.

ISOLATE THE VARIABLE
05 · HOW IT WORKS

Four basic patterns

x + 6 = 14 → subtract 6.   x − 9 = 4 → add 9.   5x = 35 → divide by 5.   x/4 = 6 → multiply by 4.

06 · WORKED EXAMPLE 1

Solve x + 11 = 19

  1. Subtract 11 from both sides.
  2. x + 11 − 11 = 19 − 11
  3. x = 8
Check: 8 + 11 = 19 ✓
07 · WORKED EXAMPLE 2

Solve x − 13 = −5

x − 13 + 13 = −5 + 13
x = 8

08 · WORKED EXAMPLE 3

A price p is reduced by $9 to $15.

p − 9 = 15
p = 24

The words “reduced by 9” translate directly to subtraction.

09 · WORKED EXAMPLE 4

Solve −6x = 42

x = 42/(−6) = −7

Check: −6(−7) = 42 ✓
10 · WORKED EXAMPLE 5

Solve x/8 = −3

8(x/8) = 8(−3)
x = −24

11 · COMMON MISTAKES

Do not change only one side. Use the inverse operation, not the same operation. Keep negative signs. For x/a = b, multiply by a rather than divide again.

12 · EXAM STRATEGY

Ask: what is stopping the variable from being alone?

Undo that one operation on both sides. In word problems, translate the relationship first, then solve.
