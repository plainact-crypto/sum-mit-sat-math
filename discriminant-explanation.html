01 · LESSON OBJECTIVE

Predict the real solutions of a quadratic without solving the whole equation.

For ax²+bx+c=0, the discriminant is D=b²−4ac. Its sign tells you how many real solutions the quadratic has.

02 · CORE RULE

Check the sign of D.

D=b²−4ac
D > 0Two distinct real solutionsThe quadratic has two different real roots.
D = 0One repeated real solutionBoth formula branches give the same root.
D < 0No real solutionsThe roots are not real numbers.
03 · WORKED EXAMPLE 1

How many real solutions does 2x²−5x+2=0 have?

a=2, b=−5, c=2D=(−5)²−4(2)(2)=25−16=9D>0 → two distinct real solutions
Math cross-check2x²−5x+2=(2x−1)(x−2), so the roots are x=1/2 and x=2: two distinct real roots.
04 · WORKED EXAMPLE 2

How many real solutions does x²−6x+9=0 have?

a=1, b=−6, c=9D=36−36=0D=0 → one repeated real solution
Math cross-checkx²−6x+9=(x−3)², so x=3 is the repeated root.
05 · WORKED EXAMPLE 3

How many real solutions does x²+4x+8=0 have?

a=1, b=4, c=8D=16−32=−16D<0 → no real solutions
Math cross-checkx²+4x+8=(x+2)²+4, which is always positive for real x, so it cannot equal zero.
06 · PARAMETER EXAMPLE

Find k so x²+kx+16=0 has one repeated real solution.

D=k²−4(1)(16)0=k²−64k²=64k=8 or k=−8
Math cross-checkFor k=8, x²+8x+16=(x+4)². For k=−8, x²−8x+16=(x−4)². Each has one repeated real root.

07 · COMMON MISTAKES
Losing the sign of b

If b is negative, square the entire signed value: for b=−5, b²=(−5)²=25.

Using −4ac incorrectly

Multiply 4, a, and c first, then subtract that product from b².

Thinking D is a solution

The discriminant classifies the roots. It is not usually one of the roots.

Forgetting standard form

Write the equation as ax²+bx+c=0 before identifying a, b, and c.

08 · EXAM STRATEGY

If the question asks only for the number or type of real roots, stop after D.

Do not spend time solving the quadratic unless the question asks for the roots themselves. Compute b²−4ac, compare it with zero, and state the conclusion clearly.

10 · LESSON RECAP
  1. Put the quadratic in standard form.
  2. Identify a, b, and c with their signs.
  3. Compute D=b²−4ac.
  4. D>0 means two distinct real roots.
  5. D=0 means one repeated real root.
  6. D<0 means no real roots.
