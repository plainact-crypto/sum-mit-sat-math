Factoring by Grouping

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

1. Factor completely: x³ + 3x² + 2x + 6

Group: x²(x+3)+2(x+3). Factor the common binomial.
Answer: A) (x + 3)(x² + 2)

2. Factor completely: x³ − 4x² + 2x − 8

Group: x²(x−4)+2(x−4). Factor (x−4).
Answer: A) (x − 4)(x² + 2)

3. Factor completely: 2x³ + 6x² + x + 3

Group: 2x²(x+3)+1(x+3).
Answer: A) (x + 3)(2x² + 1)

4. Factor: 3x³ − 6x² + 5x − 10

Group: 3x²(x−2)+5(x−2).
Answer: A) (x − 2)(3x² + 5)

5. Factor: 4x³ + 8x² − 3x − 6

Group: 4x²(x+2)−3(x+2).
Answer: A) (x + 2)(4x² − 3)

6. Factor: 6x³ − 9x² + 4x − 6

Group: 3x²(2x−3)+2(2x−3).
Answer: A) (2x − 3)(3x² + 2)

7. Factor completely: x³ + x² − 9x − 9

Group: x²(x+1)−9(x+1)=(x+1)(x²−9). Then use difference of squares.
Answer: A) (x + 1)(x − 3)(x + 3)

8. Factor: 2x³ − 8x² + 3x − 12

Group: 2x²(x−4)+3(x−4).
Answer: A) (x − 4)(2x² + 3)

9. Factor: 5a³ + 10a² + 3a + 6

Group: 5a²(a+2)+3(a+2).
Answer: A) (a + 2)(5a² + 3)

10. After reordering, factor 3m³ + 6m + 2m² + 4

Reorder to 3m³+2m²+6m+4, then group: m²(3m+2)+2(3m+2).
Answer: A) (3m + 2)(m² + 2)

11. Factor completely: x³ − 5x² − 4x + 20

Group: x²(x−5)−4(x−5)=(x−5)(x²−4), then factor the difference of squares.
Answer: A) (x − 5)(x − 2)(x + 2)

12. Which expression is equivalent to the complete factorization of 2x³ + 8x² + 3x + 12?

Group: 2x²(x+4)+3(x+4).
Answer: A) (x + 4)(2x² + 3)

13. Factor completely: x³ + 2x² − 16x − 32

Group: x²(x+2)−16(x+2)=(x+2)(x²−16). Then factor the difference of squares.
Answer: A) (x + 2)(x − 4)(x + 4)

14. If x³ + kx² + 4x + 4k factors as (x + 3)(x² + 4), what is k?

Expand the stated factorization: (x+3)(x²+4)=x³+3x²+4x+12. Comparing coefficients gives k=3 and 4k=12.
Answer: A) 3

15. Which factorization matches 6x³ + 9x² + 4x + 6?

Group: 3x²(2x+3)+2(2x+3).
Answer: A) (2x + 3)(3x² + 2)

16. Factor: 4x³ − 12x² + x − 3

Group: 4x²(x−3)+1(x−3).
Answer: A) (x − 3)(4x² + 1)

17. Factor completely: x⁵ + x⁴ − 16x − 16

Group: x⁴(x+1)−16(x+1)=(x+1)(x⁴−16). Then x⁴−16=(x²−4)(x²+4)=(x−2)(x+2)(x²+4).
Answer: A) (x + 1)(x − 2)(x + 2)(x² + 4)

18. Factor completely: 4x⁵ − 8x⁴ − 9x + 18

Group: 4x⁴(x−2)−9(x−2)=(x−2)(4x⁴−9). Then 4x⁴−9=(2x²−3)(2x²+3).
Answer: A) (x − 2)(2x² − 3)(2x² + 3)
