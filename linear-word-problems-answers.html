A gym charges a $12 registration fee plus $5 per visit. A customer pays $37 total. How many visits?

Answer: 5 visits
12 + 5v = 375v = 25v = 5Check: 12 + 5(5) = 37 ✓
2

Seven identical notebooks cost $42. What is the cost of one notebook?

Answer: $6
7n = 42n = 6Check: 7(6) = 42 ✓
3

After 14 points are deducted from a score, the result is 23. What was the original score?

Answer: 37
s − 14 = 23s = 37Check: 37 − 14 = 23 ✓

Answer: 33
