Polynomial Subtraction

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. (5x + 7) - (2x - 4)

Answer: A) 3x + 11
Distribute the subtraction: 5x+7-2x+4=3x+11.

2. (8y² + 3y) - (5y² - 2y)

Answer: A) 3y² + 5y
8y²+3y-5y²+2y=3y²+5y.

3. (6a² - a + 9) - (2a² + 4a - 3)

Answer: A) 4a² - 5a + 12
6a²-a+9-2a²-4a+3=4a²-5a+12.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. (7x³ + 2x - 5) - (3x³ - 6x + 1)

Answer: A) 4x³ + 8x - 6
7x³+2x-5-3x³+6x-1=4x³+8x-6.

5. (4m² - 7m + 2) - (-3m² + 2m - 8)

Answer: A) 7m² - 9m + 10
4m²-7m+2+3m²-2m+8=7m²-9m+10.

6. (-2p³ + 5p² - p) - (4p³ - 3p² + 6p)

Answer: A) -6p³ + 8p² - 7p
-2p³+5p²-p-4p³+3p²-6p=-6p³+8p²-7p.

7. (x⁴ - 3x² + 7) - (2x³ + x² - 5)

Answer: A) x⁴ - 2x³ - 4x² + 12
Keep missing powers aligned: x⁴-2x³-3x²-x²+7+5=x⁴-2x³-4x²+12.

8. (2.5t² - 1.2t + 4) - (0.5t² + 0.8t - 3)

Answer: A) 2t² - 2t + 7
2.5-0.5=2, -1.2-0.8=-2, and 4-(-3)=7.

9. ((3/4)x² + (1/2)x - 1) - ((1/4)x² - (1/3)x + 2)

Answer: A) (1/2)x² + (5/6)x - 3
3/4-1/4=1/2, 1/2-(-1/3)=5/6, and -1-2=-3.

10. P(x)=5x²+2x-6, Q(x)=2x²-7x+1; find P(x)-Q(x).

Answer: A) 3x² + 9x - 7
(5-2)x²+(2-(-7))x+(-6-1)=3x²+9x-7.

11. (9r⁵ - 4r³ + 2r) - (6r⁵ + r³ - 5r)

Answer: A) 3r⁵ - 5r³ + 7r
9-6=3 for r⁵, -4-1=-5 for r³, and 2-(-5)=7 for r.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. (6x² - 3x + 8) - (2x² + 5x - 4)

Answer: A) 4x² - 8x + 12
6x²-3x+8-2x²-5x+4=4x²-8x+12.

13. Tank: (5x²+4x+20) liters before (2x²-x+7) liters are used.

Answer: A) 3x² + 5x + 13 liters
Remaining means subtract used from initial: (5-2)x²+(4-(-1))x+(20-7)=3x²+5x+13 liters.

14. A(x)=3x³+2x²-5, B(x)=-2x³+7x²-4x; find A(x)-B(x).

Answer: A) 5x³ - 5x² + 4x - 5
3x³+2x²-5+2x³-7x²+4x=5x³-5x²+4x-5.

15. Target (8n²+3n+40), completed (5n²-2n+18); find still to complete.

Answer: A) 3n² + 5n + 22
Target minus completed gives 3n²+(3-(-2))n+(40-18)=3n²+5n+22.

16. P(x)-Q(x)=4x³-x²+6x-2 and P(x)=7x³+2x²+x+5; find Q(x).

Answer: A) 3x³ + 3x² - 5x + 7
Since Q=P-(P-Q), subtract the given difference: 7x³+2x²+x+5-(4x³-x²+6x-2)=3x³+3x²-5x+7.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. (5a⁴b - 2a²b² + 7ab³) - (-3a⁴b + 6a²b² - 4ab³)

Answer: A) 8a⁴b - 8a²b² + 11ab³
Subtract each matching coefficient: 5-(-3)=8, -2-6=-8, and 7-(-4)=11.

18. P(x)=(k+4)x²-3x+6, Q(x)=(k-2)x²+5x-1; simplify P(x)-Q(x).

Answer: A) 6x² - 8x + 7
The x² coefficient is (k+4)-(k-2)=6; -3-5=-8; and 6-(-1)=7. The parameter k cancels.
