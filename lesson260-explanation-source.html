Coordinate Geometry with Lines
01 · LESSON OBJECTIVE

Analyze straight lines on the coordinate plane

Use slope, intercepts, equations, distance, midpoint, parallel and perpendicular relationships, and intersections to solve SAT-style coordinate questions.

02 · WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

Coordinates describe position

An ordered pair (x, y) identifies horizontal and vertical position. Two distinct points determine one straight line.

03 · KEY DEFINITIONS

Slope, intercept, parallel, perpendicular

Slope is rise over run. Intercepts occur where a line crosses an axis. Parallel lines have equal slopes; perpendicular nonvertical lines have negative-reciprocal slopes.

04 · CORE RULE

Slope between two points

For (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂), m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁), provided x₂ ≠ x₁. A vertical line has undefined slope.

05 · HOW IT WORKS

Write an equation from a slope and a point

Use y = mx + b. Substitute the known point to find b, or use point-slope form y − y₁ = m(x − x₁).

06 · WORKED EXAMPLE 1

Calculate slope

For (−3, 1) and (1, 9), m = (9 − 1)/(1 − (−3)) = 8/4 = 2.

07 · WORKED EXAMPLE 2

Build the equation

Slope −3 through (2, 1): 1 = −3(2) + b, so b = 7 and y = −3x + 7.

08 · WORKED EXAMPLE 3

Find an intersection

Solve 2x + 1 = −x + 7 to get x = 2; substitute for y = 5. Intersection (2, 5) satisfies both equations.

09 · WORKED EXAMPLE 4

Use distance and midpoint

Between (1,2) and (4,6), distance = √(3²+4²)=5. Midpoint = ((1+4)/2,(2+6)/2)=(2.5,4).

10 · WORKED EXAMPLE 5

Find a perpendicular line

The slope perpendicular to 1/2 is −2. Through (4,1), y − 1 = −2(x − 4), giving y = −2x + 9.

12 · DESMOS STRATEGY

Use Desmos to verify line intersections

Enter y=2x+1 and y=−x+7. Look for their intersection (2,5). Use it to answer the coordinate question. Why it works: the intersection satisfies both equations. Faster or not? Graphing is a fast check; algebra gives an exact result.

13 · COMMON MISTAKES

Keep subtraction order consistent

Use the same point order in both slope differences. Do not confuse x- and y-intercepts. Parallel slopes match; perpendicular slopes multiply to −1.

14 · EXAM STRATEGY

Choose the shortest reliable tool

For a slope, subtract coordinates. For a line through a point, substitute into y=mx+b. For an intersection, set the two y-expressions equal.

15 · QUICK CHECK

Try three short questions

1) Slope through (0,1) and (2,5)? 2) Perpendicular slope to 3? 3) Intersection of y=2x+1 and y=−x+7?

Reveal quick-check answers after trying

1) 2. 2) −1/3. 3) (2,5).

16 · LESSON RECAP

Connect coordinates, slopes, and equations

Slope m = Δy/Δx. Distance uses Pythagoras; midpoint averages coordinates. Parallel slopes equal; perpendicular slopes are negative reciprocals.
