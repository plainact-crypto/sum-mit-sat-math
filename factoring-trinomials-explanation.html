Factoring Trinomials
Advanced Math
01 · LESSON OBJECTIVE

Rewrite a quadratic trinomial as a product of two binomials.

Factoring reverses multiplication. For a trinomial such as x²+7x+12, the goal is to find two binomials whose product returns the original expression.

x² + 7x + 12→(x + 3)(x + 4)
02 · CORE RULE

For x² + bx + c, find two numbers that multiply to c and add to b.

m·n = c    and    m+n = b

Then x²+bx+c=(x+m)(x+n).

03 · WORKED EXAMPLE 1 — MONIC

Factor: x² + 9x + 20

  1. Multiply target:20.
  2. Add target:9.
  3. Choose the pair:4 and 5 because 4·5=20 and 4+5=9.
  4. Write the factors:(x+4)(x+5)
Math cross-check(x+4)(x+5)=x²+5x+4x+20=x²+9x+20.
04 · WORKED EXAMPLE 2 — NEGATIVE CONSTANT

Factor: x² + x − 12

The product is negative, so the two numbers have opposite signs. We need a product of −12 and a sum of 1.

4·(−3)=−124+(−3)=1x²+x−12=(x+4)(x−3)
Math cross-check(x+4)(x−3)=x²−3x+4x−12=x²+x−12.
05 · WORKED EXAMPLE 3 — NON-MONIC

Factor: 6x² + 11x + 3

When the leading coefficient is not 1, use the AC method. Multiply a·c=6·3=18, then find two numbers that multiply to 18 and add to 11.

9·2=18 and 9+2=116x²+11x+3 = 6x²+9x+2x+3=3x(2x+3)+1(2x+3)=(3x+1)(2x+3)
Math cross-check(3x+1)(2x+3)=6x²+9x+2x+3=6x²+11x+3.
06 · WORKED EXAMPLE 4 — TAKE OUT THE GCF FIRST

Factor completely: 3x² + 18x + 24

3x²+18x+24 = 3(x²+6x+8)2·4=8 and 2+4=6=3(x+2)(x+4)

Always check for a GCF first. Otherwise the final answer may not be completely factored.

07 · SIGN PATTERNS

c positive, b positive

Both factor numbers are positive.

x²+7x+12=(x+3)(x+4)

c positive, b negative

Both factor numbers are negative.

x²−7x+12=(x−3)(x−4)

c negative

The factor numbers have opposite signs.

x²+x−12=(x+4)(x−3)
08 · RELIABLE METHOD
  1. Factor out any GCF.
  2. Identify a, b, and c.
  3. If a=1, find two numbers with product c and sum b.
  4. If a≠1, find two numbers with product ac and sum b.
  5. Split the middle term and factor by grouping.
  6. Multiply the factors back to verify every coefficient.
09 · ROOTS AND FACTORS

Factors also reveal the zeros of the related quadratic equation.

If x²+7x+12=(x+3)(x+4), then solving x²+7x+12=0 gives x=-3 or x=-4. This connection makes a graphing tool useful for checking a factorization.

Math cross-checkAt x=−3: 9−21+12=0. At x=−4: 16−28+12=0.
10 · COMMON MISTAKES
Using only the product condition

The pair must satisfy both the product and the sum.

Missing the GCF

Factor the common factor before working on the trinomial.

Forgetting ac when a≠1

For 6x²+11x+3, search for a pair multiplying to 18, not 3.

Not checking by multiplication

A quick expansion catches sign and coefficient errors immediately.

11 · EXAM STRATEGY

Use structure before trial and error.

For a monic trinomial, list factor pairs of c and stop when the sum matches b. For a non-monic trinomial, use the AC method systematically. In a multiple-choice question, multiplying the proposed factors can be faster than refactoring from scratch.

13 · LESSON RECAP
  1. Factoring reverses multiplication.
  2. For x²+bx+c, match product c and sum b.
  3. For ax²+bx+c, the AC method uses product ac and sum b.
  4. Factor out the GCF first.
  5. Multiply back to verify the factorization.
  6. Zeros from the factors provide an independent check.
