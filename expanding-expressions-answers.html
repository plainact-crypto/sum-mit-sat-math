Expanding Expressions

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. Expand: 2(x + 6)

Answer: A) 2x + 12
Distribute 2 to both terms: 2·x + 2·6 = 2x + 12.

2. Expand: 4(a - 3)

Answer: A) 4a - 12
4·a + 4·(-3) = 4a - 12.

3. Expand: -3(y + 5)

Answer: A) -3y - 15
The negative factor multiplies both terms: -3·y + (-3)·5 = -3y - 15.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. Simplify: 3(x + 2) + 2x

Answer: A) 5x + 6
Expand to 3x+6+2x, then combine like terms: 5x+6.

5. Simplify: 5(m - 4) - 2m

Answer: A) 3m - 20
5m-20-2m = 3m-20.

6. Simplify: -2(3p - 7) + p

Answer: A) -5p + 14
-6p+14+p = -5p+14.

7. Simplify: (1/2)(10t - 8) + 2t

Answer: A) 7t - 4
Half of 10t-8 is 5t-4; adding 2t gives 7t-4.

8. Simplify: 0.5(6r + 4) - r

Answer: A) 2r + 2
0.5(6r+4)=3r+2, and 3r+2-r=2r+2.

9. Simplify: 2(3x + 1) - 4(x - 2)

Answer: A) 2x + 10
6x+2-4x+8 = 2x+10.

10. Simplify: -(4a - 9) + 3(a + 1)

Answer: A) -a + 12
-4a+9+3a+3 = -a+12.

11. Simplify: 3[2q - (q - 5)]

Answer: A) 3q + 15
Inside the brackets, 2q-(q-5)=q+5. Multiply by 3 to get 3q+15.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. Rectangle with length 2x + 3 and width 5

Answer: A) 10x + 15
Area = 5(2x+3)=10x+15 square units.

13. Equivalent to 4(2x - 3) - (x + 5)

Answer: A) 7x - 17
8x-12-x-5 = 7x-17.

14. If 2(3x + k) - x = 5x + 14 for every x, find k

Answer: B) 7
The left side expands to 5x+2k. Matching constants gives 2k=14, so k=7. Substitution gives 2(3x+7)-x=5x+14.

15. Cost of n visits after a $2 discount from $12 per visit

Answer: A) 10n
Each visit costs 12-2=$10, so n visits cost 10n dollars.

16. Simplify: 3(2y - 1) - 2(1 - y)

Answer: A) 8y - 5
6y-3-2+2y = 8y-5.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. Simplify: -2[3x - 2(4 - x)] + 5x

Answer: A) -5x + 16
Inside, 3x-2(4-x)=3x-8+2x=5x-8. Then -2(5x-8)+5x=-10x+16+5x=-5x+16.

18. Simplify: (1/3)[6z - 3(2 - z)] - (z - 4)

Answer: A) 2z + 2
Inside the brackets, 6z-3(2-z)=9z-6. One third gives 3z-2; subtracting z-4 gives 3z-2-z+4=2z+2.
