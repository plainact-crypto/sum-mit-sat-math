Linear Inequality Word Problems

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. Reading goal

Answer: A) x ≥ 52
18+x≥70, so x≥52. At least 52 additional pages are needed.

2. Notebook budget

Answer: C) 3 + 6n ≤ 45
The $3 delivery fee plus $6 per notebook cannot exceed $45, so 3+6n≤45.

3. Copier packets

Answer: B) 11
68+12p≤200 gives 12p≤132 and p≤11. The greatest whole number is 11.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. Taxi miles

Answer: C) 10
5+2.5m≤30 gives 2.5m≤25, so m≤10.

5. Savings goal

Answer: C) 11
75+15w≥240 gives 15w≥165, so w≥11. The least whole number is 11.

6. Seating rows

Answer: B) 10
34+8r≤120 gives 8r≤86, so r≤10.75. Only whole rows count, so the maximum is 10.

7. Extra data

Answer: A) g > 7
22+4g>50 gives 4g>28, so g>7.

8. Ribbon gifts

Answer: B) 10
4.5+2.5g≤30 gives 2.5g≤25.5, so g≤10.2. The greatest whole number is 10.

9. Movie rentals

Answer: B) m < 12
12+1.5m<30 gives 1.5m<18, so m<12.

10. Fundraiser batches

Answer: B) 6
48+22b≥180 gives 22b≥132, so b≥6.

11. Rectangle length

Answer: A) x < 18
2x+2(7)<50 gives 2x<36, so x<18 cm.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. Suitcase books

Answer: B) 13
4.2+1.35b≤23 gives b≤18.8/1.35≈13.93. The greatest whole number is 13.

13. Account withdrawals

Answer: B) 6
286−31w≥100 gives −31w≥−186. Dividing by −31 reverses the sign: w≤6.

14. Passing score

Answer: B) x ≥ 82
338+x≥420, so x≥82 points.

15. Ride distance

Answer: B) 15 km
8.5+3.7d≤64 gives 3.7d≤55.5, so d≤15 km.

16. Profit target

Answer: B) at least 39
Profit is 38k−18k−260=20k−260. Require 20k−260>500, so 20k>760 and k>38. For whole kits, at least 39 must be sold.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. Cooling sample

Answer: B) at most 4
29−3h≥17 gives −3h≥−12. Reverse the sign when dividing by −3: h≤4.

18. Prepaid months

Answer: A) 5
120−17.5m>15 gives −17.5m>−105. Dividing by −17.5 reverses the sign: m<6. The greatest whole number is 5.
