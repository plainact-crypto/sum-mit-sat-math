Slope-Intercept Form
Algebra
01 · LESSON OBJECTIVE

Read a line directly from y = mx + b.

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to identify the slope and y-intercept of a linear equation written in slope-intercept form, interpret both quantities in context, and use them to recognize or build a line.

y = mx + b→m = slope, b = y-intercept
02 · WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

Slope-intercept form exposes the two most useful features of a line.

In y = mx + b, the coefficient m tells you how much y changes when x increases by 1. The constant b tells you where the line crosses the y-axis.

Formy = mx + bLinear equation solved for y.
SlopemChange in y for each 1-unit increase in x.
Y-interceptbThe y-value when x = 0.
Intercept point(0, b)Where the line crosses the y-axis.
03 · KEY DEFINITIONS

Slope

The rate of vertical change compared with horizontal change.

m = Δy / Δx

Y-Intercept

The y-coordinate of the point where the line crosses the y-axis.

x = 0 → y = b

Initial Value

In many real-world models, b is the amount present before x begins changing.

y = 4x + 12 → initial value = 12
04 · CORE RULE

In y = mx + b, read m from the x-term and b from the constant.

SLOPE = m · Y-INTERCEPT = b
05 · HOW IT WORKS

Read slope-intercept form in four moves

01
Make sure y is isolated.The equation must look like y = something.
02
Find the coefficient of x.That number is the slope m.
03
Find the constant term.That number is the y-intercept b.
04
Turn b into a point when needed.The y-intercept point is (0, b).
06 · WORKED EXAMPLE 1 — BASIC

Find the slope and y-intercept of y = 3x + 5.

  1. Compare with y = mx + b.y = 3x + 5
  2. Read the x coefficient.m = 3, so the slope is 3.
  3. Read the constant.b = 5, so the y-intercept is 5.
Answerslope = 3; y-intercept = 5, or point (0, 5) ✓
07 · WORKED EXAMPLE 2 — NEGATIVE SLOPE

Find m and b in y = −2x + 7.

m = −2b = 7y-intercept = (0, 7)

Interpretation: Each time x increases by 1, y decreases by 2.

08 · WORKED EXAMPLE 3 — FRACTIONAL SLOPE

Read the features of y = (3/4)x − 6.

m = 3/4b = −6y-intercept = (0, −6)

A slope of 3/4 means y rises 3 units for every 4 units of horizontal increase.

09 · WORKED EXAMPLE 4 — NO WRITTEN CONSTANT

What are m and b in y = 5x?

y = 5x + 0m = 5b = 0
ConclusionThe line passes through the origin, (0, 0).
10 · WORKED EXAMPLE 5 — EXAM STYLE

A streaming service charges a one-time setup fee of $18 plus $9 per month. If x is the number of months and y is the total cost, write the equation and interpret its slope and y-intercept.

monthly charge = 9xinitial fee = 18y = 9x + 18
Exam connection

The slope 9 is the cost added for each month. The y-intercept 18 is the starting cost when x = 0. In test-style questions, interpreting the rate and initial value is often more important than simply naming m and b.

VISUAL GRAPH

See m and b directly on the line.

For y = 3x − 4, the line crosses the y-axis at (0, −4). Increasing x by 1 increases y by 3, so the slope is 3.

Math cross-checkx = 0 gives y = −4; x = 1 gives y = −1; x = 2 gives y = 2. The rise from −4 to −1 over a run of 1 is 3, confirming m = 3 and b = −4.
DESMOS STRATEGY

Use a graphing tool to verify slope and intercept.

Enter

y = 3x - 4

Look for

The y-axis crossing at (0, −4) and a rise of 3 for every run of 1.

Use it to answer

Confirm that m = 3 and b = −4.

Why it works

In y = mx + b, b is the output at x = 0 and m is the constant rate of change.

Faster or not?

Reading m and b directly from slope-intercept form is faster; Desmos is useful for visual verification.

Math cross-checkSubstitution gives (0, −4), (1, −1), and (2, 2), and (−1 − (−4))/(1 − 0) = 3.
11 · SPECIAL CASES

The missing pieces still have values.

No constant showny = 4xb = 0.
No x-term showny = 6m = 0 and b = 6.
Negative constanty = 2x − 5b = −5.
Coefficient 1y = x + 3m = 1.
12 · COMMON MISTAKES
Reading b as a point instead of a value

If b = 4, the y-intercept value is 4 and the intercept point is (0, 4).

Losing the sign of the slope

In y = −3x + 2, m = −3.

Using the x-intercept as b

b is specifically the y-intercept, found where x = 0.

Reading m before isolating y

If the equation is not already in y = mx + b form, rearrange it first before identifying m and b.

13 · EXAM STRATEGY

Translate m as “per” and b as “starts at.”

For a real-world linear model, the coefficient of x usually represents a rate such as dollars per item, miles per hour, or points per game. The constant usually represents an initial amount, fixed fee, starting height, or value at x = 0.

y = 6x + 40→+6 per unit · starts at 40

15 · LESSON RECAP

Slope-intercept form tells you how a line changes and where it starts.

  1. Use the pattern y = mx + b.
  2. m is the slope.
  3. b is the y-intercept value.
  4. The y-intercept point is (0, b).
  5. A positive slope rises left to right; a negative slope falls.
  6. In context, m is usually a rate and b is usually an initial value.
y = mx + bm = slope(0, b) = y-intercept
