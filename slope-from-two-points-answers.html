Slope from Two Points

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. What is the slope of the line through (2, 3) and (6, 11)?

Answer: B) 2
m = (11 − 3)/(6 − 2) = 8/4 = 2.

2. What is the slope of the line through (−1, 4) and (3, −8)?

Answer: B) −3
m = (−8 − 4)/(3 − (−1)) = −12/4 = −3.

3. What is the slope of the line through (0, −5) and (10, 0)?

Answer: B) 1/2
m = (0 − (−5))/(10 − 0) = 5/10 = 1/2.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. Find the slope through (1, 7) and (5, 19).

Answer: B) 3
m = (19 − 7)/(5 − 1) = 12/4 = 3.

5. Find the slope through (−4, 2) and (2, 14).

Answer: B) 2
m = (14 − 2)/(2 − (−4)) = 12/6 = 2.

6. Find the slope through (3, 8) and (9, 2).

Answer: B) −1
m = (2 − 8)/(9 − 3) = −6/6 = −1.

7. What is the slope through (−2, −3) and (4, −3)?

Answer: B) 0
The y-values are equal, so Δy = 0. Therefore m = 0/(4 − (−2)) = 0.

8. What is the slope through (5, −1) and (5, 9)?

Answer: D) undefined
The x-values are equal, so Δx = 0. A vertical line has undefined slope.

9. Find the slope through (0.5, 4) and (2.5, 10).

Answer: B) 3
m = (10 − 4)/(2.5 − 0.5) = 6/2 = 3.

10. Find the slope through (−3, 7) and (1, −1).

Answer: B) −2
m = (−1 − 7)/(1 − (−3)) = −8/4 = −2.

11. Find the slope through (2, 5) and (8, 8).

Answer: B) 1/2
m = (8 − 5)/(8 − 2) = 3/6 = 1/2.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. A delivery fee is $9 when the trip distance is 2 miles and $21.50 when the distance is 7 miles. If the relationship is linear, what is the rate of change of cost with respect to distance?

Answer: B) $2.50 per mile
Rate = (21.50 − 9)/(7 − 2) = 12.50/5 = $2.50 per mile.

13. A tank contains 44 liters after 3 minutes and 20 liters after 11 minutes. What is the average rate of change in volume over that interval?

Answer: B) −3 L/min
Rate = (20 − 44)/(11 − 3) = −24/8 = −3 L/min.

14. A line passes through (−6, 5) and (2, −7). What is its slope?

Answer: A) −3/2
m = (−7 − 5)/(2 − (−6)) = −12/8 = −3/2.

15. A line passes through (2, 6) and (8, k). Its slope is 4/3. What is k?

Answer: C) 14
4/3 = (k − 6)/(8 − 2) = (k − 6)/6. Thus k − 6 = 8, so k = 14.

16. A line passes through (−2, 1) and (4, 10). Which statement is true?

Answer: D) Its slope is 3/2
m = (10 − 1)/(4 − (−2)) = 9/6 = 3/2.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. The slope between (p, 5) and (7, 17) is 3. What is p?

Answer: C) 3
3 = (17 − 5)/(7 − p) = 12/(7 − p). So 3(7 − p) = 12, giving 7 − p = 4 and p = 3.

18. Points A(−4, 11), B(2, −1), and C(8, y) lie on the same line. What is y?

Answer: B) −13
Slope AB = (−1 − 11)/(2 − (−4)) = −12/6 = −2. From B to C, Δx = 6, so Δy = −2(6) = −12. Therefore y = −1 − 12 = −13.
