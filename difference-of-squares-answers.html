Difference of Squares

Solutions correspond exactly to Practice Questions 1–18.

01 · SKILL CHECK

1. Factor completely: x² − 25

Answer: A) (x − 5)(x + 5)
Use a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b). Here x² − 25 = x² − 5², so the factors are (x − 5)(x + 5). Expanding gives x² − 25.

2. Factor completely: 49 − y²

Answer: A) (7 − y)(7 + y)
Since 49 = 7², write 7² − y² = (7 − y)(7 + y).

3. Factor completely: 9a² − 16

Answer: A) (3a − 4)(3a + 4)
Both terms are perfect squares: 9a² = (3a)² and 16 = 4². Apply the difference-of-squares pattern.
02 · CORE PRACTICE

4. Factor: 4x² − 81

Answer: A) (2x − 9)(2x + 9)
4x² − 81 = (2x)² − 9², so it factors as conjugates (2x − 9)(2x + 9).

5. Factor: 100m² − 1

Answer: A) (10m − 1)(10m + 1)
100m² = (10m)² and 1 = 1². Therefore 100m² − 1 = (10m − 1)(10m + 1).

6. Factor completely: 3x² − 75

Answer: A) 3(x − 5)(x + 5)
First factor out the GCF 3: 3(x² − 25). Then x² − 25 = (x − 5)(x + 5).

7. Factor completely: 8p² − 72

Answer: A) 8(p − 3)(p + 3)
Factor out 8 to get 8(p² − 9). Since p² − 9 = p² − 3², the complete factorization is 8(p − 3)(p + 3).

8. Factor: 25r⁴ − 36s²

Answer: A) (5r² − 6s)(5r² + 6s)
25r⁴ = (5r²)² and 36s² = (6s)². Their difference factors into the conjugate pair shown.

9. Factor completely: x⁴ − 16

Answer: A) (x − 2)(x + 2)(x² + 4)
First, x⁴ − 16 = (x² − 4)(x² + 4). Then factor x² − 4 again: (x − 2)(x + 2). Over the integers, x² + 4 does not factor further.

10. Which expression is a difference of squares?

Answer: A) 16x² − 49
16x² − 49 = (4x)² − 7², a difference of two perfect squares. The other choices either use addition, include a middle term, or do not have both terms as perfect squares.

11. Factor completely: 81u⁴ − v⁴

Answer: A) (3u − v)(3u + v)(9u² + v²)
Start with (9u²)² − (v²)² = (9u² − v²)(9u² + v²). Then 9u² − v² = (3u − v)(3u + v). The sum 9u² + v² does not factor over the integers.
03 · EXAM-STYLE PRACTICE

12. A square has area 64x² square units and a smaller square has area 25 square units. Factor the difference of their areas.

Answer: A) (8x − 5)(8x + 5)
The areas are (8x)² and 5². Their difference is 64x² − 25 = (8x − 5)(8x + 5).

13. If x² − k² = (x − 12)(x + 12), what is the positive value of k?

Answer: A) 12
The identity (x − k)(x + k) = x² − k² matches (x − 12)(x + 12), so k = 12. Expanding confirms x² − 144.

14. Factor completely: 18x² − 50

Answer: A) 2(3x − 5)(3x + 5)
Factor out 2: 18x² − 50 = 2(9x² − 25). Then 9x² − 25 = (3x − 5)(3x + 5).

15. For what positive value of c does 36x² − c factor as (6x − 7)(6x + 7)?

Answer: A) 49
Expanding the conjugates gives (6x)² − 7² = 36x² − 49, so c = 49.

16. The area of a rectangular frame is modeled by (x + 9)² − (x − 9)². Which simplified expression gives the area?

Answer: A) 36x
Treat the two binomials as A and B: A² − B² = (A − B)(A + B). Here A − B = 18 and A + B = 2x, so the product is 18(2x) = 36x.
04 · CHALLENGE PROBLEMS

17. Factor completely over the integers: x⁸ − 256

Answer: A) (x − 2)(x + 2)(x² + 4)(x⁴ + 16)
x⁸ − 256 = (x⁴ − 16)(x⁴ + 16). Next, x⁴ − 16 = (x² − 4)(x² + 4) = (x − 2)(x + 2)(x² + 4). Thus the full integer factorization is (x − 2)(x + 2)(x² + 4)(x⁴ + 16).

18. For positive integer n, factor completely: x^(4n) − 81

Answer: A) (x^n − 3)(x^n + 3)(x^(2n) + 9)
Write x^(4n) − 81 = (x^(2n))² − 9² = (x^(2n) − 9)(x^(2n) + 9). Then x^(2n) − 9 = (x^n − 3)(x^n + 3), giving the stated factorization.
